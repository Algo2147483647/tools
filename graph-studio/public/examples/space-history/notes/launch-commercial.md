# Launch and commercial space

Launch demonstrations, reusable spacecraft and commercial transport.

[Workspace guide](../README.md) · [Sources and date conventions](../SOURCES.md)

All 25 events and phases in this theme are included in **space-history.json**. Choose **Launch and commercial space** in the Type filter to focus on them within the same timeline.

## STS-1

Columbia's first flight demonstrated a winged, reusable orbital spacecraft with a runway landing.

**Interval:** Shuttle orbital test flight, 1981-04-12 to 1981-04-14.

- **1981-04-12** — Columbia launches
- **1981-04-14** — Edwards runway landing

[Source 1](https://www.nasa.gov/mission/sts-1/)

## STS-135

Atlantis delivered supplies to the International Space Station on the last Space Shuttle mission.

**Interval:** Final Shuttle flight, 2011-07-08 to 2011-07-21.

- **2011-07-08** — Atlantis launches
- **2011-07-10** — ISS docking
- **2011-07-21** — Final Shuttle landing

[Source 1](https://www.nasa.gov/mission/sts-135/)

## Dragon C2+

SpaceX's Dragon became the first commercial spacecraft to be captured and berthed at the ISS. Capture and berthing are distinct from autonomous docking.

**Interval:** Commercial cargo demonstration, 2012-05-22 to 2012-05-31.

- **2012-05-22** — Falcon 9 / Dragon launch
- **2012-05-25** — ISS crew captures Dragon
- **2012-05-31** — Dragon splashdown

[Source 1](https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/presrep2012.pdf)

## Crew Dragon Demo-2

Robert Behnken and Douglas Hurley tested commercial crew transport to the ISS.

**Interval:** Crewed demonstration flight, 2020-05-30 to 2020-08-02.

- **2020-05-30** — Crew Dragon launches
- **2020-05-31** — ISS docking
- **2020-08-02** — Gulf of Mexico splashdown

[Source 1](https://www.nasa.gov/nasas-spacex-demo-2-resources-archive/)

## Artemis I

The first SLS flight sent Orion around the Moon to test integrated systems and high-speed reentry.

**Interval:** Uncrewed lunar test flight, 2022-11-16 to 2022-12-11.

- **2022-11-16** — SLS maiden launch
- **2022-11-25** — Distant retrograde orbit insertion
- **2022-12-11** — Orion splashdown

[Source 1](https://www.nasa.gov/mission/artemis-i/)

## Blue Ghost Mission 1

Firefly's commercial lander delivered NASA instruments to Mare Crisium under the CLPS program.

**Interval:** Flight to lunar landing, 2025-01-15 to 2025-03-02.

- **2025-01-15** — Launch
- **2025-03-02** — Mare Crisium landing

[Source 1](https://fireflyspace.com/missions/blue-ghost-mission-1/)

## Additional milestones

- **1979-12-24 — Ariane 1 maiden launch.** Europe's Ariane launcher made its first flight from French Guiana. [Source](https://www.esa.int/Enabling_Support/Space_Transportation/Ariane/The_origins_of_Ariane)
- **2008-09-28 — Falcon 1 reaches orbit.** The fourth Falcon 1 flight successfully reached Earth orbit. [Source](https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/presrep2008.pdf)
- **2015-12-22 — Falcon 9 lands at Cape Canaveral.** An orbital-class Falcon 9 first stage landed at Cape Canaveral. The UTC date was December 22; Florida was still on December 21. [Source](https://blogs.nasa.gov/commercialcrew/2015/12/)
