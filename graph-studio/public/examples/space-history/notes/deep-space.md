# Deep space

Gravity assists, outer planets, comet rendezvous and asteroid samples.

[Workspace guide](../README.md) · [Sources and date conventions](../SOURCES.md)

All 59 events and phases in this theme are included in **space-history.json**. Choose **Deep space** in the Type filter to focus on them within the same timeline.

## Mariner 2

The first successful planetary flyby measured Venus's hot environment without an imaging camera.

**Interval:** Flight through Venus encounter, 1962-08-27 to 1963-01-03.

- **1962-08-27** — Launch
- **1962-12-14** — Venus flyby
- **1963-01-03** — Last contact

[Source 1](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/mariner-2/)

## Mariner 10

A Venus gravity assist sent the spacecraft toward three flybys of Mercury.

**Interval:** Flight through Mercury encounters, 1973-11-03 to 1975-03-16.

- **1973-11-03** — Launch
- **1974-02-05** — Venus gravity assist
- **1974-03-29** — First Mercury flyby
- **1974-09-21** — Second Mercury flyby
- **1975-03-16** — Third Mercury flyby

[Source 1](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/mariner-10/)

## Pioneer 10

The first Jupiter flyby tested passage through the asteroid belt and returned close-range measurements.

**Interval:** Flight to Jupiter, 1972-03-03 to 1973-12-04.

- **1972-03-03** — Launch (UTC)
- **1972-07-15** — Enters asteroid belt
- **1973-12-04** — Jupiter flyby

[Source 1](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/pioneer-10/) · [Source 2](https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/chronology.html)

## Pioneer 11

A Jupiter gravity assist redirected Pioneer 11 toward the first Saturn flyby.

**Interval:** Flight through giant-planet encounters, 1973-04-06 to 1979-09-01.

- **1973-04-06** — Launch
- **1974-12-03** — Jupiter flyby
- **1979-09-01** — Saturn flyby

[Source 1](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/pioneer-11/) · [Source 2](https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/chronology.html)

## Voyager 1

The trajectory encountered Jupiter and Saturn before the later interstellar mission. This interval ends at Saturn, not at spacecraft retirement.

**Interval:** Planetary encounter phase, 1977-09-05 to 1980-11-12.

- **1977-09-05** — Launch
- **1979-03-05** — Jupiter flyby
- **1980-11-12** — Saturn flyby

[Source 1](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/voyager/voyager-1/)

## Voyager 2

The only spacecraft to encounter all four giant planets. The plotted phase ends at Neptune; the spacecraft continued outward.

**Interval:** Grand-tour encounter phase, 1977-08-20 to 1989-08-25.

- **1977-08-20** — Launch
- **1979-07-09** — Jupiter flyby
- **1981-08-25** — Saturn flyby
- **1986-01-24** — Uranus flyby
- **1989-08-25** — Neptune flyby

[Source 1](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/voyager/voyager-2/)

## Galileo

An atmospheric probe and orbiter investigated Jupiter and its moons; the orbiter was deliberately sent into Jupiter at mission end.

**Interval:** Jupiter exploration flight, 1989-10-18 to 2003-09-21.

- **1989-10-18** — Launch aboard Atlantis
- **1991-10-29** — Gaspra asteroid flyby
- **1993-08-28** — Ida asteroid flyby
- **1995-12-07** — Jupiter arrival and probe entry
- **2003-09-21** — Orbiter enters Jupiter

[Source 1](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/galileo/)

## Cassini-Huygens

The NASA/ESA/ASI mission combined a long-lived Saturn orbiter with ESA's Huygens probe to Titan.

**Interval:** Saturn exploration flight, 1997-10-15 to 2017-09-15.

- **1997-10-15** — Launch
- **2004-07-01** — Saturn orbit insertion (UTC)
- **2004-12-25** — Huygens released
- **2005-01-14** — Huygens lands on Titan
- **2017-09-15** — Cassini's final atmospheric entry

[Source 1](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/cassini/)

## Rosetta

Rosetta escorted comet 67P and deployed the Philae lander before the orbiter's controlled descent.

**Interval:** Comet rendezvous flight, 2004-03-02 to 2016-09-30.

- **2004-03-02** — Launch
- **2014-08-06** — Arrives at comet 67P
- **2014-11-12** — Philae reaches the comet surface
- **2016-09-30** — Orbiter's controlled comet descent

[Source 1](https://www.esa.int/Science_Exploration/Space_Science/Rosetta)

## New Horizons

Jupiter's gravity assist preceded the Pluto and Arrokoth flybys. The interval is a completed encounter phase, not a claim that the mission ended.

**Interval:** Flight through Arrokoth encounter, 2006-01-19 to 2019-01-01.

- **2006-01-19** — Launch
- **2007-02-28** — Jupiter gravity assist
- **2015-07-14** — Pluto flyby
- **2019-01-01** — Arrokoth flyby

[Source 1](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/new-horizons/)

## Hayabusa2

Japan's spacecraft studied Ryugu and returned a sample capsule, then continued into an extended mission. Earth return was December 5 UTC, December 6 in Japan.

**Interval:** Ryugu sample-return phase, 2014-12-03 to 2020-12-05.

- **2014-12-03** — Launch
- **2018-06-27** — Arrives at Ryugu
- **2020-12-05** — Sample capsule returns (UTC)

[Source 1](https://www.isas.jaxa.jp/en/missions/spacecraft/current/hayabusa2.html) · [Source 2](https://global.jaxa.jp/press/2020/12/20201206-1_e.html)

## OSIRIS-REx

A sample collection from Bennu was returned to Earth; the spacecraft continued as OSIRIS-APEX after releasing its capsule.

**Interval:** Bennu sample-return phase, 2016-09-08 to 2023-09-24.

- **2016-09-08** — Launch
- **2018-12-03** — Arrives at Bennu
- **2020-10-20** — Touch-and-go sampling
- **2023-09-24** — Sample capsule lands in Utah

[Source 1](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/osiris-rex/)
