# Observatories and solar science

Space telescopes, solar exploration and completed science phases.

[Workspace guide](../README.md) · [Sources and date conventions](../SOURCES.md)

All 21 events and phases in this theme are included in **space-history.json**. Choose **Observatories and solar science** in the Type filter to focus on them within the same timeline.

## Kepler

Transit photometry surveyed exoplanets; the repurposed K2 campaign extended the spacecraft's observing life.

**Interval:** Science mission through retirement, 2009-03-07 to 2018-10-30.

- **2009-03-07** — Launch (UTC)
- **2018-10-30** — Mission retirement announced

[Source 1](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/kepler/)

## Spitzer

Infrared observations continued in a warm mission after the cryogenic phase ended.

**Interval:** Infrared observatory operations, 2003-08-25 to 2020-01-30.

- **2003-08-25** — Launch
- **2009-05-15** — Liquid helium depleted
- **2020-01-30** — Science operations end

[Source 1](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/spitzer/)

## Herschel

A far-infrared and submillimetre observatory operated until its helium coolant was exhausted.

**Interval:** Cryogenic observatory operations, 2009-05-14 to 2013-04-29.

- **2009-05-14** — Launch with Planck
- **2013-04-29** — Helium exhaustion ends observations

[Source 1](https://www.esa.int/Science_Exploration/Space_Science/Herschel)

## James Webb Space Telescope

The NASA/ESA/CSA infrared observatory deployed en route to L2 and completed commissioning before its first full-colour science release.

**Interval:** Launch and commissioning, 2021-12-25 to 2022-07-12.

- **2021-12-25** — Ariane 5 launch
- **2022-01-24** — L2 orbit insertion
- **2022-07-12** — First full-colour science release

[Source 1](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/webb/)

## Parker Solar Probe

Repeated Venus gravity assists enabled increasingly close passages to the Sun. The named interval ends at the December 2024 encounter, not at mission retirement.

**Interval:** Flight to record solar approach, 2018-08-12 to 2024-12-24.

- **2018-08-12** — Launch
- **2024-12-24** — Record close solar approach

[Source 1](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/parker-solar-probe/)

## Additional milestones

- **1990-04-24 — Hubble launches aboard Discovery.** The NASA/ESA observatory was carried into orbit by STS-31. [Source](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/hubble/overview/hubble-timeline/)
- **1993-12-02 — Hubble's first servicing mission launches.** STS-61 carried optical correction instruments and other upgrades to Hubble. [Source](https://www.nasa.gov/mission/sts-61/)
- **1999-07-23 — Chandra launches aboard Columbia.** STS-93 deployed the X-ray observatory for high-energy astronomy. [Source](https://www.nasa.gov/mission/sts-93/)
- **1999-12-10 — XMM-Newton launches.** ESA's X-ray observatory launched on Ariane 5. [Source](https://www.esa.int/Science_Exploration/Space_Science/XMM-Newton_factsheet)
