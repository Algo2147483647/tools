# Neural networks and deep learning

The neural line begins before the AI label. Formal neurons establish computational possibilities; Hebbian ideas concern learning through connectivity. Perceptrons and ADALINE provide trainable decisions, while critiques reveal limits of particular architectures. The history of backpropagation includes reverse-mode differentiation, early learning applications and later popularization.

Associative and probabilistic networks, recurrent models, convolutional systems and deep pretraining develop partly in parallel. AlexNet is a meeting of these research traditions with data and compute. Residual connections, sparse experts and alternative sequence architectures continue to address trainability, capacity and efficiency.

Generative models add another branch: VAEs, GANs and diffusion learn distributions or latent structure. Their relevance to AGI lies in reusable representations, simulation and action generation, but plausible samples are not evidence of complete world understanding. Inspect the cross-links to language, reinforcement learning and embodiment to see how neural components enter larger systems.
