# Symbolic reasoning planning and knowledge

Logic Theorist and GPS treat problem solving as structured search. Resolution and Prolog supply executable inference; STRIPS represents action; frames and nonmonotonic logic address missing or revisable background information. Expert systems then exploit specialist knowledge to constrain otherwise expensive search.

The line does not end with the expert-system market contraction. Automated theorem proving, AlphaGeometry, AlphaProof and formalization use explicit rules or checkers to validate proposals, including proposals made by neural models. Symbolic systems can be precise where a formal representation exists; acquiring the representation and choosing useful search steps remain major challenges.

Read the modern convergence through two roles: a learned model proposes, while an external formal process checks or searches. This is a technical combination, not a resolution of every philosophical dispute between symbolic and connectionist accounts.
