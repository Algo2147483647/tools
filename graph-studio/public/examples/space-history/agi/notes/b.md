# Embodiment and world models

Shakey integrates sensing, planning and action. Behavior-based robotics challenges the need for a complete centralized model before acting. Probabilistic and active-inference accounts take other routes through uncertainty and prediction. Practical autonomous vehicles illustrate the importance of integrating many components.

Modern world models learn compressed dynamics that can support imagined experience or planning. Dreamer, generative interactive worlds and joint-embedding prediction do not build the same kind of model: some emphasize latent control, others visual generation, others abstract predictive representation. Their evaluations must match the intended role.

SayCan explicitly connects linguistic suggestions with executable skills. RT-1, RT-2, pi0 and Gemini Robotics investigate broader learned policies. Across these systems, the decisive questions include embodiment changes, action consequences, data coverage and recovery. The map does not equate a convincing video with an accurate causal simulator.
