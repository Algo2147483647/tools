# Language and multimodal foundation models

Statistical language modeling and formal grammar provide different early approaches to linguistic structure. ELIZA reveals how conversational form can suggest understanding even with a restricted mechanism. Statistical translation, neural word representations, recurrent sequence models and attention gradually make more linguistic behavior learnable from data.

Transformer pretraining supports several branches: bidirectional encoders, autoregressive generators and multimodal models. GPT-3 makes in-context task specification prominent. Scaling laws guide resource choices, while Chinchilla shows why more parameters alone are not a complete training strategy.

Instruction tuning, dialogue interfaces and image-audio-text integration make broad models accessible and adaptable. Their role in AGI research is the reusable base and task interface, not a guarantee that every skill appears through text prediction. Follow the edges outward to tools, robotic action, alignment and tests of new-skill acquisition.
