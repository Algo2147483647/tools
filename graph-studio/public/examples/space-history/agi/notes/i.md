# Data compute and research institutions

Research institutions and shared infrastructure change which hypotheses can be tested. Dartmouth gives an emerging agenda a name; the Fifth Generation project ties knowledge processing to a national computing program; the AGI conference sustains explicit whole-system ambitions.

ImageNet, GPU programming and shared robotic datasets alter experimental scale. DeepMind, OpenAI, Anthropic and other laboratories organize sustained research with differing emphases. Released model weights widen access, but weight access, licensing, training-data openness and reproducible pretraining are separate properties.

The recent automation of parts of AI research returns to an old self-improvement question in a measurable setting. The timeline distinguishes AI assistance, work led under human supervision and fully autonomous successor creation. Organization-level reports provide evidence about a particular setting, not universal measures of the entire field.
