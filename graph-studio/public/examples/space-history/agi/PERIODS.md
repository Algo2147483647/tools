# Research periods and programs

The timeline contains **25 duration bars**: **5 research programs** and **20 editorial research phases**, alongside **231 point milestones**.

Program bars describe projects or bounded development efforts. Phase bars synthesize overlapping changes in methods and research emphasis. Their boundaries are reading aids, not globally agreed dates at which a school began or disappeared. Recent phases stop at the October 9, 2026 evidence cutoff; that is a snapshot boundary, not a claim that the work ended.

Use **Type → Research phase** for the broad development map or **Type → Research program** for project durations. **All types** combines the bars and milestones. Select a bar to highlight its related milestones, and double-click it for evidence and boundary notes.

The **part-of** connector runs from a grouping interval to a milestone. Its attachment to the bar uses the milestone date, where the milestone lies within that interval. A milestone can belong to more than one phase. The original milestone-to-milestone relationships remain available.

## Research program intervals

### Dartmouth summer research project

**1956-06 to 1956-08** · Data compute and research institutions

An extended summer study brings changing groups of researchers together to explore machine intelligence. The 1955 proposal and the 1956 working period are separate historical objects.

**Why this interval matters.** The shared research agenda takes institutional form over weeks of discussion and work, rather than at one instantaneous founding ceremony.

**Boundary convention.** Approximate June-August 1956 window, at month precision. Solomonoff's archive contains notes from June through August; these are not exact opening and closing dates or a claim that everyone attended throughout.

**Scope and limits.** Attendance varied, and the participants did not agree on a finished architecture or solve general intelligence.

[Source 1](https://www-formal.stanford.edu/jmc/history/dartmouth/dartmouth.html) · [Source 2](https://raysolomonoff.com/dartmouth/boxbdart/boxbdart.html)

### Shakey research and development

**1966 to 1972** · Embodiment and world models

The SRI project integrates visual perception, world representation, planning and mobile action through repeated engineering and experiments. Search and planning methods develop within this sustained effort.

**Why this interval matters.** Embodied intelligence becomes an integration problem involving several interacting components, with identifiable technical results produced during the project.

**Boundary convention.** SRI dates Shakey development to 1966-1972. Both boundaries retain year precision; they are project dates, not the lifetime of the underlying research ideas.

**Scope and limits.** The controlled rooms and tasks differ substantially from open-world robotics; the project did not establish unrestricted general intelligence.

[Source 1](https://www.sri.com/case-study/the-man-the-myth-the-legend-meet-shakey-the-robot-the-worlds-first-ai-based-robot/)

### Fifth Generation Computer Systems project

**1982 to 1992** · Data compute and research institutions

A decade-long national research program develops logic-programming-based parallel inference systems, associated architectures and software. Its 1982 launch remains a separate point event.

**Why this interval matters.** Symbolic inference is pursued through a coordinated combination of hardware, programming language and application research.

**Boundary convention.** ICOT states that the main FGCS project ran from 1982 to its conclusion in 1992. The later follow-on effort and March 1995 closure are not folded into this bar.

**Scope and limits.** Project completion does not establish general intelligence. The main project must be distinguished from the subsequent software-portability and dissemination work.

[Source 1](https://www.airc.aist.go.jp/aitec-icot/ICOT/Museum/ICRA/FGCSProject/mou_termination_inria_9411.html)

### IBM chess research through the Deep Blue victory

**1989 to 1997-05-11** · Reinforcement learning search and self-play

After researchers join IBM in 1989, chess-specific search, evaluation and specialized computing develop through successive systems and matches, culminating in the May 1997 Kasparov rematch.

**Why this interval matters.** The victory becomes the endpoint of a sustained engineering program rather than an isolated event, clarifying the role of search, domain knowledge and computing resources.

**Boundary convention.** The start is the 1989 move to IBM described in its institutional history. The end is the final match day, not an asserted exact project shutdown date.

**Scope and limits.** This is the IBM development-to-victory window. Earlier ChipTest and Deep Thought work predates it; chess strength alone does not establish general intelligence.

[Source 1](https://www.ibm.com/history/deep-blue)

### CALO cognitive-assistant research program

**2003 to 2008** · Cognitive architectures and general intelligence

The Cognitive Assistant that Learns and Organizes combines learning, reasoning, planning, interaction and personal information management in a sustained integration effort.

**Why this interval matters.** A practical assistant becomes a testbed for combining cognitive capabilities and learning from a user over time, well before contemporary language-model agents.

**Boundary convention.** SRI describes five years of collaborative work beginning in 2003; 2003-2008 is the resulting year-level project window. Personnel, publication and commercialization timelines can extend beyond it.

**Scope and limits.** Office-assistant tasks constrain its scope. Later Siri commercialization is a related transfer history, not evidence that the research assistant achieved AGI.

[Source 1](https://www.sri.com/75-years-of-innovation/75-years-of-innovation-calo-cognitive-assistant-that-learns-and-organizes/)
