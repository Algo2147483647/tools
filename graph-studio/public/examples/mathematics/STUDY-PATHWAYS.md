# Study pathways

Use the overview to choose a field, then open its subject graph. These pathways select material for different goals; they are not complete degree requirements.

## Analysis and stochastic processes

Real analysis → functional analysis and complex analysis; measure and integration → probability → conditional expectation → martingales → Brownian stochastic calculus. Revisit uniform integrability before passing expectations through stopping-time limits.

## Algebra and arithmetic

Groups and actions → quotient structures → Galois theory; commutative rings → algebraic integers → prime ideals and class groups → completions. Complex analysis supplies the analytic route to prime distribution. Elliptic curves also require the geometry of smooth projective curves.

## Geometry and topology

Point-set topology → homotopy and homology → smooth manifolds → differential forms and de Rham theory → Riemannian curvature. Functional analysis provides analytic tools for Hodge theory. Commutative algebra supplies a separate entrance to schemes, divisors and algebraic curves.

## Foundations

Sets and first-order syntax → semantics → soundness and completeness → compactness. Computability and arithmetic coding lead to incompleteness. Ordinals, choice and ZF lead to relative consistency and forcing. Logical completeness and incompleteness of an arithmetic theory address different questions.

## Cross-field connections

- [Real analysis](./guides/real-analysis.md) → [Complex analysis](./guides/complex-analysis.md): Metric limits and uniform convergence support holomorphic function theory.
- [Real analysis](./guides/real-analysis.md) → [Functional analysis](./guides/functional-analysis.md): Integration and Lp spaces supply the principal infinite-dimensional examples.
- [Real analysis](./guides/real-analysis.md) → [Probability theory](./guides/probability-theory.md): Probability is normalized measure theory with additional independence structure.
- [Topology](./guides/topology.md) → [Differential geometry](./guides/differential-geometry.md): Topological manifolds acquire smooth atlases, forms and metrics.
- [Group theory](./guides/group-theory.md) → [Number theory](./guides/number-theory.md): Group actions and Galois theory organize arithmetic symmetry.
- [Group theory](./guides/group-theory.md) → [Algebraic geometry](./guides/algebraic-geometry.md): Quotients and algebraic structures lead to rings, modules and coordinate algebras.
- [Complex analysis](./guides/complex-analysis.md) → [Algebraic geometry](./guides/algebraic-geometry.md): Riemann surfaces connect complex analysis with smooth projective curves over the complex numbers.
- [Functional analysis](./guides/functional-analysis.md) → [Differential geometry](./guides/differential-geometry.md): Elliptic operators connect Sobolev estimates with Hodge theory.

## How to read an entry

First identify its kind and level. Copy the hypotheses before using a theorem. Work through the example, test the warning against a counterexample, and reconstruct the proof idea from the reference. A prerequisite arrow is selected pedagogical background, not a theorem asserting necessity or logical implication.
