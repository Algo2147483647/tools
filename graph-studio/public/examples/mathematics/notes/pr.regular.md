# Regular conditional distributions

Probability theory · Theorem · Core

Stable ID: `pr.regular`

## Statement

For random elements X,Y in standard Borel spaces, the conditional law of X given Y admits a probability-kernel version K(y,A) satisfying the conditional integral identities.

## Hypotheses and conventions

A standard Borel space is measurably isomorphic to a Borel subset of a Polish space.

## Example

Conditional densities, when available, give concrete kernels.

## Scope and common pitfalls

Versions can differ on Y-null sets; arbitrary measurable spaces need not admit such kernels.

## Proof idea

Construct conditional probabilities on a countable determining family and extend to measures.

This is a proof strategy; details and intermediate lemmas are in the references.
