# Tychonoff theorem

Topology · Theorem · Core

Stable ID: `tp.tychonoff`

## Statement

An arbitrary product of compact spaces is compact in the product topology.

## Hypotheses and conventions

This form is stated in ZFC; the corresponding general theorem is equivalent to choice over ZF.

## Example

Products of closed bounded intervals are compact, including products with infinitely many factors.

## Scope and common pitfalls

Using the box topology instead invalidates the general conclusion.

## Proof idea

Extend a family with the finite-intersection property to an ultrafilter and use convergence in every coordinate.

This is a proof strategy; details and intermediate lemmas are in the references.
