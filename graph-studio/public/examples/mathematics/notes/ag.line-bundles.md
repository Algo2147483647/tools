# Line bundles and the Picard group

Algebraic geometry · Definition · Core

Stable ID: `ag.line-bundles`

Rank-one locally free sheaves form the Picard group under tensor product.

## Statement

Invertible sheaves are locally free modules of rank one over the structure sheaf. Tensor product gives their isomorphism classes the group $\operatorname{Pic}(X)$. A Cartier divisor D defines $\mathcal O_X(D)$.

## Hypotheses and conventions

The inverse line bundle is its dual; on an integral scheme Cartier divisors modulo principal divisors give the Picard group.

## Example

On projective space the bundles $\mathcal O(m)$ measure degree.

## Scope and common pitfalls

A line bundle may be locally trivial but globally nontrivial.
