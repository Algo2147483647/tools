# Hodge theorem

Differential geometry · Theorem · Advanced gateway

Stable ID: `dg.hodge`

On a closed oriented Riemannian manifold, cohomology classes have unique harmonic representatives.

## Statement

On a closed oriented Riemannian manifold, each real de Rham cohomology class has exactly one harmonic representative, annihilated by $\Delta=dd^*+d^*d$.

## Hypotheses and conventions

Compactness without boundary and the Riemannian metric are part of this statement.

## Example

The dimension of harmonic k-forms equals the kth real Betti number.

## Scope and common pitfalls

Manifolds with boundary require boundary conditions, and noncompact versions require analytic qualifications.

## Proof idea

Elliptic estimates and compactness give an orthogonal decomposition into harmonic, exact and coexact forms.

This is a proof strategy; details and intermediate lemmas are in the references.
