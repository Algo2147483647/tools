# Weil and Cartier divisors

Algebraic geometry · Definition · Core

Stable ID: `ag.divisors`

## Statement

On a Noetherian normal integral scheme, a Weil divisor is a finite integral sum of codimension-one integral closed subschemes. A Cartier divisor is locally a nonzero rational function modulo invertible regular functions.

## Hypotheses and conventions

Cartier divisors define Weil divisors in this setting; they coincide on locally factorial schemes, including regular Noetherian integral schemes.

## Example

On a smooth curve a divisor is a finite integer-weighted sum of closed points.

## Scope and common pitfalls

On singular normal varieties, not every Weil divisor is Cartier.
