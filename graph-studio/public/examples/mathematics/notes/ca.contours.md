# Contour integrals and winding number

Complex analysis · Construction · Core

Stable ID: `ca.contours`

Winding number measures the signed turns of a closed contour around a point.

## Statement

For a piecewise $C^1$ closed curve $\gamma$ and $a$ off its image, $\operatorname{Ind}(\gamma,a)=(2\pi i)^{-1}\int_\gamma dz/(z-a)$ is an integer.

## Hypotheses and conventions

Orientation and the parametrized curve, including repeated traversal, matter.

## Example

A positively oriented circle winds once around its center and zero times around an exterior point.

## Scope and common pitfalls

Being geometrically enclosed is inadequate for self-intersecting or multiply traversed curves.
