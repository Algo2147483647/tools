# Trace pairing and discriminants

Number theory · Definition · Core

Stable ID: `nt.discriminant`

The trace pairing defines an arithmetic invariant of an integral basis.

## Statement

A basis $\omega_i$ has discriminant $\det(\operatorname{Tr}(\omega_i\omega_j))$. An integral basis defines the basis-independent field discriminant $d_K$.

## Hypotheses and conventions

A suborder's discriminant is the field discriminant times the square of its index.

## Example

$d_{\mathbb Q(\sqrt5)}=5$.

## Scope and common pitfalls

Polynomial and field discriminants can differ.
