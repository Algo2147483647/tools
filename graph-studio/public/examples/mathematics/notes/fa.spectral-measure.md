# Spectral theorem for bounded normal operators

Functional analysis · Theorem · Advanced gateway

Stable ID: `fa.spectral-measure`

Bounded normal operators have a projection-valued spectral representation.

## Statement

A bounded normal operator on a complex Hilbert space admits a projection-valued measure E with $T=\int_{\sigma(T)}z\,dE(z)$ and a bounded Borel functional calculus.

## Hypotheses and conventions

Normality means $TT^*=T^*T$.

## Example

Multiplication by the coordinate function on L2 can have continuous spectrum without an eigenbasis.

## Scope and common pitfalls

The compact eigenvector formulation does not describe every bounded normal operator.

## Proof idea

Represent the commutative operator algebra by continuous functions and then extend the calculus to spectral projections.

This is a proof strategy; details and intermediate lemmas are in the references.
