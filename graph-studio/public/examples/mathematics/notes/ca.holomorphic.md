# Holomorphic functions

Complex analysis · Definition · Core

Stable ID: `ca.holomorphic`

Complex differentiability throughout an open set imposes strong local rigidity.

## Statement

A function on an open set $U\subset\mathbb C$ is holomorphic if the complex derivative exists at every point of $U$. This is stronger than real differentiability.

## Hypotheses and conventions

The derivative limit must be independent of the direction of approach.

## Example

$z^2$ is holomorphic; $\bar z$ is not holomorphic on any nonempty open set.

## Scope and common pitfalls

Complex differentiability at one isolated point does not imply local holomorphy.
