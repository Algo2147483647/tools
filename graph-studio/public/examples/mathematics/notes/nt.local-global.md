# Local–global principles and limits

Number theory · Theorem · Advanced gateway

Stable ID: `nt.local-global`

## Statement

A nondegenerate quadratic form over a number field has a nonzero zero if and only if it has one over every completion.

## Hypotheses and conventions

The Hasse–Minkowski theorem includes every finite and Archimedean place.

## Example

Projective conics can be tested over completions.

## Scope and common pitfalls

Genus-one torsors can have points over every completion but no rational point.

## Proof idea

Local classification of quadratic forms combines with global reciprocity; the global-to-local direction is immediate.

This is a proof strategy; details and intermediate lemmas are in the references.
