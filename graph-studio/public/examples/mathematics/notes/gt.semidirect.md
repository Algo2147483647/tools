# Semidirect products and split extensions

Group theory · Construction · Core

Stable ID: `gt.semidirect`

An action assembles two groups into a split extension.

## Statement

An action $\varphi:H\to\operatorname{Aut}(N)$ defines $N\rtimes_\varphi H$ by $(n,h)(n',h')=(n\varphi(h)(n'),hh')$.

## Hypotheses and conventions

The action by automorphisms is part of the data.

## Example

Dihedral groups combine rotations with a reflection acting by inversion.

## Scope and common pitfalls

A short exact sequence need not split; a normal subgroup and quotient do not determine the extension.
