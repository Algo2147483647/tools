# Mittag–Leffler theorem

Complex analysis · Theorem · Advanced gateway

Stable ID: `ca.mittag-leffler`

## Statement

On a plane domain, arbitrary prescribed finite principal parts at a discrete set of points are realized by a meromorphic function with no other poles.

## Hypotheses and conventions

The prescribed set has no accumulation point inside the domain.

## Example

Prescribing simple poles with residue one at the integers leads to meromorphic partial-fraction constructions.

## Scope and common pitfalls

On compact Riemann surfaces analogous prescriptions have global obstructions; unrestricted plane-domain existence does not transfer.

## Proof idea

Correct successive finite sums by holomorphic approximants to obtain convergence on compact exhaustions.

This is a proof strategy; details and intermediate lemmas are in the references.
