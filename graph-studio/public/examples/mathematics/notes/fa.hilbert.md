# Hilbert spaces and orthogonal projection

Functional analysis · Theorem · Core

Stable ID: `fa.hilbert`

## Statement

A Hilbert space is complete in its inner-product norm. Every vector has a unique orthogonal decomposition relative to a closed linear subspace.

## Hypotheses and conventions

The subspace must be closed; use real or complex inner products consistently.

## Example

Least-squares approximation projects onto a finite-dimensional span.

## Scope and common pitfalls

Projection onto a nonclosed subspace may fail to exist.

## Proof idea

Minimize distance using the parallelogram identity to make a minimizing sequence Cauchy.

This is a proof strategy; details and intermediate lemmas are in the references.
