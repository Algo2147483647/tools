# Geodesics and the exponential map

Differential geometry · Definition · Core

Stable ID: `dg.geodesics`

Geodesics have parallel velocity and define the local exponential map.

## Statement

A geodesic satisfies $\nabla_{\dot\gamma}\dot\gamma=0$. The exponential map sends an initial vector v to the value at time one of its geodesic, when defined.

## Hypotheses and conventions

Local existence and uniqueness follow from the smooth geodesic ODE.

## Example

Great circles are geodesics of the round sphere.

## Scope and common pitfalls

Geodesics minimize distance locally, but may cease to minimize globally past a cut point.
