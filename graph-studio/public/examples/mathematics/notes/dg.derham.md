# de Rham theorem

Differential geometry · Theorem · Core

Stable ID: `dg.derham`

Differential forms compute real singular cohomology by integration.

## Statement

Integration of closed forms over smooth singular cycles induces $H^k_{\mathrm{dR}}(M)\cong H^k(M;\mathbb R)$.

## Hypotheses and conventions

M is a smooth manifold; the comparison is with real singular cohomology.

## Example

A closed one-form on a circle has a nontrivial class when its period is nonzero.

## Scope and common pitfalls

Real differential forms do not detect integral torsion classes.

## Proof idea

Use the local Poincare lemma and a Mayer–Vietoris or sheaf argument to pass from local to global.

This is a proof strategy; details and intermediate lemmas are in the references.
