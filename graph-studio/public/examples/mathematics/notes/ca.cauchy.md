# Cauchy–Goursat theorem

Complex analysis · Theorem · Core

Stable ID: `ca.cauchy`

Holomorphic functions have zero closed-contour integrals on simply connected domains.

## Statement

If $f$ is holomorphic on a simply connected domain $U$, then $\int_\gamma f(z)\,dz=0$ for every closed piecewise $C^1$ curve in $U$.

## Hypotheses and conventions

The entire curve lies in the domain. Holomorphy does not require an a priori continuous derivative.

## Example

The integral of $1/z$ around the unit circle is nonzero because the punctured plane is not simply connected.

## Scope and common pitfalls

The general homological version requires a null-homologous cycle in the domain.

## Proof idea

Prove the triangle theorem by subdivision, then construct primitives locally and use simple connectivity.

This is a proof strategy; details and intermediate lemmas are in the references.
