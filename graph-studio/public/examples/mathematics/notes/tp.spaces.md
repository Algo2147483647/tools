# Topological spaces and continuity

Topology · Definition · Core

Stable ID: `tp.spaces`

## Statement

A topology contains the empty set and the whole space, is closed under arbitrary unions and finite intersections. A map is continuous when inverse images of open sets are open.

## Hypotheses and conventions

Open sets, rather than a chosen metric, are the primitive data.

## Example

The discrete and indiscrete topologies put very different structures on the same underlying set.

## Scope and common pitfalls

Sequential continuity need not characterize continuity in arbitrary spaces.
