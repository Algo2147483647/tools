# Montel's theorem

Complex analysis · Theorem · Core

Stable ID: `ca.normal`

## Statement

A locally uniformly bounded family of holomorphic functions on a plane domain has a subsequence converging uniformly on compact subsets from every sequence in the family.

## Hypotheses and conventions

The locally bounded version yields a finite holomorphic limit; the compact-open topology is intended.

## Example

Holomorphic disk maps with values in $\mathbb D$ form a normal family.

## Scope and common pitfalls

Pointwise boundedness is not the hypothesis, and meromorphic normality uses the spherical metric.

## Proof idea

Cauchy estimates give equicontinuity on compact subsets; apply compactness and a diagonal exhaustion.

This is a proof strategy; details and intermediate lemmas are in the references.
