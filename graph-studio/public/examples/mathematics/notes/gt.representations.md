# Linear representations

Group theory · Definition · Core

Stable ID: `gt.representations`

Linear representations realize a group by invertible linear transformations.

## Statement

A representation is a homomorphism $G\to\operatorname{GL}(V)$, equivalently a module over the group algebra. It is irreducible when V has no nonzero proper invariant subspace.

## Hypotheses and conventions

Fix the scalar field and whether the representation is finite-dimensional.

## Example

The regular representation acts by translation on a basis indexed by G.

## Scope and common pitfalls

Irreducible does not mean faithful.
