# CW complexes

Topology · Construction · Core

Stable ID: `tp.cw`

## Statement

A CW complex is built inductively by attaching disks along their boundary spheres, with the closure-finite and weak-topology conditions.

## Hypotheses and conventions

Attaching maps need not be embeddings.

## Example

A circle has one 0-cell and one 1-cell; a torus has a standard finite cell structure.

## Scope and common pitfalls

A cell decomposition is extra structure and is not unique.
