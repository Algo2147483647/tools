# Tangent spaces and differentials

Differential geometry · Definition · Core

Stable ID: `dg.tangent`

Tangent vectors are derivations, and smooth maps differentiate by the chain rule.

## Statement

A tangent vector at p is a derivation on germs of smooth functions at p. A smooth map f induces $df_p:T_pM\to T_{f(p)}N$ with $d(g\circ f)_p=dg_{f(p)}\circ df_p$.

## Hypotheses and conventions

Derivations are linear and satisfy the Leibniz rule.

## Example

A curve through p gives a tangent vector by differentiating functions along it.

## Scope and common pitfalls

A tangent vector is intrinsic; a chosen coordinate tuple is its representation.
