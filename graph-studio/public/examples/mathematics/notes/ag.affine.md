# Affine varieties and coordinate rings

Algebraic geometry · Construction · Core

Stable ID: `ag.affine`

Affine geometry is encoded contravariantly by coordinate rings.

## Statement

For an affine algebraic set X over an algebraically closed field, $k[X]=k[x_1,\ldots,x_n]/I(X)$. Regular maps correspond contravariantly to k-algebra homomorphisms of coordinate rings.

## Hypotheses and conventions

Here variety means a reduced irreducible finite-type object, so its coordinate ring is a domain; reducible algebraic sets are also allowed when named as such.

## Example

The affine parabola has coordinate ring $k[x,y]/(y-x^2)\cong k[x]$.

## Scope and common pitfalls

A polynomial expression in chosen coordinates is a presentation, not the intrinsic object.
