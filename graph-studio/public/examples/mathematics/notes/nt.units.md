# Dirichlet's unit theorem

Number theory · Theorem · Core

Stable ID: `nt.units`

The unit group has a finite torsion part and a rank determined by embeddings.

## Statement

If K has $r_1$ real embeddings and $r_2$ conjugate pairs of complex embeddings, $\mathcal O_K^\times\cong\mu_K\times\mathbb Z^{r_1+r_2-1}$.

## Hypotheses and conventions

The finite group of roots of unity is the torsion subgroup.

## Example

$\mathbb Z[\sqrt2]^\times=\{\pm(1+\sqrt2)^m:m\in\mathbb Z\}$.

## Scope and common pitfalls

Computing fundamental units is a separate problem from determining their rank.

## Proof idea

The logarithmic embedding and geometry of numbers yield a full lattice in a hyperplane.

This is a proof strategy; details and intermediate lemmas are in the references.
