# Mordell–Weil theorem

Number theory · Theorem · Advanced gateway

Stable ID: `nt.mordell-weil`

Rational points on an elliptic curve over a number field are finitely generated.

## Statement

For an elliptic curve E over a number field K, $E(K)$ is a finitely generated abelian group.

## Hypotheses and conventions

E is nonsingular and K is a number field.

## Example

$E(K)\cong E(K)_{\mathrm{tors}}\oplus\mathbb Z^r$ for finite r.

## Scope and common pitfalls

Finite generation does not mean finitely many rational points or an easy rank computation.

## Proof idea

Finite descent classes and height reduction establish finite generation.

This is a proof strategy; details and intermediate lemmas are in the references.
