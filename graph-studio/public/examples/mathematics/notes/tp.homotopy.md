# Homotopies and homotopy equivalence

Topology · Definition · Core

Stable ID: `tp.homotopy`

Homotopies deform maps continuously; homotopy equivalences preserve algebraic invariants.

## Statement

Maps f,g are homotopic when a continuous $H:X\times[0,1]\to Y$ interpolates between them. A homotopy equivalence has an inverse up to homotopy.

## Hypotheses and conventions

For based constructions, specify which basepoints are fixed throughout.

## Example

A punctured plane deformation retracts onto a circle.

## Scope and common pitfalls

Homotopy equivalence is weaker than homeomorphism.
