# Distributions and weak derivatives

Real analysis · Definition · Advanced gateway

Stable ID: `ra.distributions`

Test-function duality extends differentiation beyond ordinary functions.

## Statement

A distribution on an open $U\subset\mathbb R^n$ is a continuous linear functional on $C_c^\infty(U)$ with its test-function topology. Define $\langle D^\alpha T,\phi\rangle=(-1)^{|\alpha|}\langle T,D^\alpha\phi\rangle$.

## Hypotheses and conventions

The test-function topology controls all derivatives on a fixed compact support along convergent sequences.

## Example

The distributional derivative of the Heaviside function is the Dirac mass.

## Scope and common pitfalls

A distribution need not be an ordinary function, and arbitrary distributions cannot generally be multiplied.
