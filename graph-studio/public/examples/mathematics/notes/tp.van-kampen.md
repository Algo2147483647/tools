# Seifert–van Kampen theorem

Topology · Theorem · Core

Stable ID: `tp.van-kampen`

Compatible open decompositions compute fundamental groups by a group pushout.

## Statement

If $X=U\cup V$ with U,V and their intersection path connected and U,V open, $\pi_1(X)$ is the pushout of $\pi_1(U)$ and $\pi_1(V)$ over $\pi_1(U\cap V)$.

## Hypotheses and conventions

Choose the basepoint in the intersection; disconnected intersections require a more general formulation.

## Example

A bouquet of two circles has free fundamental group on two generators.

## Scope and common pitfalls

An arbitrary closed cover does not meet these stated hypotheses.

## Proof idea

Subdivide loops and homotopies along the cover, then identify exactly the intersection relations.

This is a proof strategy; details and intermediate lemmas are in the references.
