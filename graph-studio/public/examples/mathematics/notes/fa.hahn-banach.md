# Hahn–Banach extension theorem

Functional analysis · Theorem · Core

Stable ID: `fa.hahn-banach`

## Statement

A bounded linear functional on a linear subspace of a normed space extends to the entire space with unchanged norm.

## Hypotheses and conventions

The extension is scalar-valued; the real sublinear-domination version yields the complex normed-space statement as well.

## Example

A nonzero vector can be separated from zero by a norm-one continuous functional.

## Scope and common pitfalls

Arbitrary vector-valued operators need not admit norm-preserving extensions.

## Proof idea

Extend one dimension at a time under a domination bound and use a maximal extension argument.

This is a proof strategy; details and intermediate lemmas are in the references.
