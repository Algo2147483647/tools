# Gauss–Bonnet for closed surfaces

Differential geometry · Theorem · Core

Stable ID: `dg.gauss-bonnet`

Total Gaussian curvature of a closed oriented surface is determined by its Euler characteristic.

## Statement

For a closed oriented Riemannian surface M, $\int_M K\,dA=2\pi\chi(M)$.

## Hypotheses and conventions

K is Gaussian curvature; surfaces with boundary require geodesic-curvature and corner terms.

## Example

The unit sphere has total curvature 4 pi and Euler characteristic two.

## Scope and common pitfalls

Pointwise curvature depends on the metric even though this total is topological.

## Proof idea

Integrate the local curvature identity on a triangulation and sum angle defects; internal boundary terms cancel.

This is a proof strategy; details and intermediate lemmas are in the references.
