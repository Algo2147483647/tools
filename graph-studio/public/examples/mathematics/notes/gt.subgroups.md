# Subgroups and generated subgroups

Group theory · Definition · Core

Stable ID: `gt.subgroups`

Subgroups inherit the operation; generators specify the smallest containing subgroup.

## Statement

A nonempty subset H of G is a subgroup when $xy^{-1}\in H$ for all x,y in H. The subgroup generated by S is the intersection of subgroups containing S.

## Hypotheses and conventions

The ambient group operation is inherited.

## Example

Every subgroup of the additive integers is $n\mathbb Z$ for a unique $n\ge0$.

## Scope and common pitfalls

Closure under multiplication alone need not suffice for an infinite subset.
