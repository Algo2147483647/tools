# Regularity and smoothness

Algebraic geometry · Definition · Core

Stable ID: `ag.regular`

Regular local rings compare dimension with tangent size; smoothness is relative.

## Statement

A Noetherian local ring is regular when $\dim A=\dim_{\kappa}(\mathfrak m/\mathfrak m^2)$. For a finite-type scheme over a perfect field, regularity is equivalent to smoothness over that field.

## Hypotheses and conventions

Smoothness is a relative notion; the perfect-field hypothesis matters for this comparison.

## Example

The cusp has a larger tangent space than its local dimension at the singular point.

## Scope and common pitfalls

Over an imperfect field, regular need not mean geometrically regular or smooth over that field.
