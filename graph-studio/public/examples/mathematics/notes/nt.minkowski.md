# Minkowski's theorem and finite class number

Number theory · Theorem · Core

Stable ID: `nt.minkowski`

Large symmetric convex bodies contain lattice points, bounding ideal class representatives.

## Statement

A centrally symmetric convex measurable body in $\mathbb R^n$ of volume greater than $2^n\operatorname{covol}(\Lambda)$ contains a nonzero point of the full lattice $\Lambda$. This implies finiteness of $\operatorname{Cl}(K)$.

## Hypotheses and conventions

Strict inequality avoids boundary subtleties for nonclosed bodies.

## Example

Real and complex embeddings of ideals produce explicit bounds for ideal class representatives.

## Scope and common pitfalls

Convexity and central symmetry are essential to this formulation.

## Proof idea

Apply a lattice pigeonhole argument to half the body, then bound norms in inverse ideal classes.

This is a proof strategy; details and intermediate lemmas are in the references.
