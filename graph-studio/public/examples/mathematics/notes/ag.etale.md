# Étale morphisms

Algebraic geometry · Definition · Advanced gateway

Stable ID: `ag.etale`

## Statement

An etale morphism is smooth of relative dimension zero, equivalently flat, unramified and locally of finite presentation.

## Hypotheses and conventions

Etale is a relative condition and includes a finiteness requirement.

## Example

A finite separable field extension induces an etale map of spectra.

## Scope and common pitfalls

In positive characteristic the Frobenius of an affine line is not etale.
