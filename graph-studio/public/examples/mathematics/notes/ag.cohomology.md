# Sheaf cohomology

Algebraic geometry · Construction · Core

Stable ID: `ag.cohomology`

Sheaf cohomology measures the failure of global sections to preserve exactness.

## Statement

Sheaf cohomology $H^i(X,\mathcal F)$ is the right derived functor of global sections. For affine X and quasi-coherent $\mathcal F$, $H^i(X,\mathcal F)=0$ for $i>0$.

## Hypotheses and conventions

The vanishing statement is for quasi-coherent sheaves, not arbitrary sheaves; derived functors are taken in the abelian sheaf category.

## Example

Cohomology measures obstructions to solving gluing problems globally.

## Scope and common pitfalls

A single arbitrary Cech cover does not always compute derived-functor cohomology.
