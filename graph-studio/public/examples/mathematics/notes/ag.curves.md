# Smooth projective curves and genus

Algebraic geometry · Definition · Core

Stable ID: `ag.curves`

The genus of a smooth projective curve is a cohomological invariant.

## Statement

For a smooth projective geometrically integral curve C over k, its genus is $g=\dim_k H^1(C,\mathcal O_C)$. The canonical line bundle is $\omega_C=\Omega^1_{C/k}$.

## Hypotheses and conventions

Geometric integrality ensures the curve remains integral after field extension.

## Example

The projective line has genus zero; a smooth plane cubic has genus one.

## Scope and common pitfalls

Arithmetic and geometric genus may differ for singular curves.
