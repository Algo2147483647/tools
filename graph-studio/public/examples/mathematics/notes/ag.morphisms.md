# Scheme morphisms and fiber products

Algebraic geometry · Construction · Core

Stable ID: `ag.morphisms`

Fiber products express base change and are built affinely by tensor products.

## Statement

A ring map $A\to B$ induces $\operatorname{Spec}B\to\operatorname{Spec}A$. Fiber products satisfy the universal property of simultaneous maps and, affinely, use tensor products: $\operatorname{Spec}B\times_{\operatorname{Spec}A}\operatorname{Spec}C=\operatorname{Spec}(B\otimes_A C)$.

## Hypotheses and conventions

The base scheme and structure morphisms are part of the data.

## Example

Extension of the ground field is base change.

## Scope and common pitfalls

Topological fiber products alone lose the structure sheaf and multiplicities.
