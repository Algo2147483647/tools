# Axiom of choice, Zorn and well-ordering

Set theory and mathematical logic · Theorem · Core

Stable ID: `sl.choice`

## Statement

Over ZF, the axiom of choice, the well-ordering theorem and Zorn's lemma are equivalent. Zorn's lemma says that a nonempty partially ordered set in which every chain has an upper bound has a maximal element.

## Hypotheses and conventions

A maximal element need not be a greatest element.

## Example

Zorn's lemma yields a basis for every vector space.

## Scope and common pitfalls

Countable and dependent choice are weaker principles and should not be silently identified with full choice.

## Proof idea

Choice builds maximal chains by transfinite methods; suitable partial choice functions recover choice from Zorn.

This is a proof strategy; details and intermediate lemmas are in the references.
