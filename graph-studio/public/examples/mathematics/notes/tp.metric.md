# Metric and Polish spaces

Topology · Definition · Core

Stable ID: `tp.metric`

## Statement

A metric induces open balls and hence a topology. A Polish space is separable and completely metrizable: some compatible metric is complete.

## Hypotheses and conventions

Completeness depends on the metric; complete metrizability is a property of the topology.

## Example

An open interval is Polish although its usual Euclidean metric is not complete.

## Scope and common pitfalls

Not every topological space is metrizable.
