# Vector fields, Lie brackets and flows

Differential geometry · Definition · Core

Stable ID: `dg.flows`

Vector fields generate local flows; their commutators define the Lie bracket.

## Statement

A vector field acts as a derivation on smooth functions. Its Lie bracket is $[X,Y]f=X(Yf)-Y(Xf)$. Local flows solve the associated ordinary differential equation.

## Hypotheses and conventions

Local existence and uniqueness use smoothness; global flows require completeness of the vector field.

## Example

Every smooth vector field on a compact manifold without boundary is complete.

## Scope and common pitfalls

A vector field on a noncompact manifold may escape in finite time.
