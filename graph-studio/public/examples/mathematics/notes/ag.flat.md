# Flatness

Algebraic geometry · Definition · Advanced gateway

Stable ID: `ag.flat`

Flat modules preserve exact sequences under tensoring.

## Statement

An A-module M is flat if $-\otimes_A M$ preserves exact sequences. A scheme morphism $f:X\to Y$ is flat when $\mathcal O_{X,x}$ is flat over $\mathcal O_{Y,f(x)}$ for every x in X.

## Hypotheses and conventions

For a morphism X to Y, stalks of X are modules over the corresponding stalks of Y.

## Example

Localizations and free modules are flat.

## Scope and common pitfalls

Flatness alone does not force every fiber to be smooth.
