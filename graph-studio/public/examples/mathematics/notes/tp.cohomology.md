# Cohomology and cup product

Topology · Definition · Core

Stable ID: `tp.cohomology`

Cohomology adds a graded product to the additive invariants of a space.

## Statement

Applying $\operatorname{Hom}(-,R)$ to chains gives cohomology $H^*(X;R)$. The cup product makes it a graded ring with $ab=(-1)^{pq}ba$ for degrees p,q and commutative coefficients.

## Hypotheses and conventions

Integral cohomology is not always just the dual of integral homology; Ext terms can appear.

## Example

The cup product distinguishes spaces with identical additive cohomology groups.

## Scope and common pitfalls

Chain-level cup products need not be strictly graded commutative before passing to cohomology.
