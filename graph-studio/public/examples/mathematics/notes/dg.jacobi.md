# Jacobi fields

Differential geometry · Theorem · Advanced gateway

Stable ID: `dg.jacobi`

Jacobi fields describe first-order separation of nearby geodesics.

## Statement

A variation through geodesics has variation field J satisfying $\nabla_t^2J+R(J,\dot\gamma)\dot\gamma=0$. Conversely local Jacobi fields arise from suitable geodesic variations.

## Hypotheses and conventions

The curvature convention is the one specified in the curvature entry; derivatives are covariant.

## Example

On the round sphere initially diverging meridians reconverge at the antipode.

## Scope and common pitfalls

A conjugate point and a cut point are related but not identical notions.

## Proof idea

Differentiate the geodesic equation along the variation and commute the two covariant derivatives.

This is a proof strategy; details and intermediate lemmas are in the references.
