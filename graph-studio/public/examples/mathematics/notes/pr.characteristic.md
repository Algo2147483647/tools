# Characteristic functions

Probability theory · Definition · Core

Stable ID: `pr.characteristic`

Fourier transforms of probability laws encode distributions and independent sums.

## Statement

The function $\varphi_X(t)=\mathbb E e^{itX}$ determines the law of a real variable. Independence turns characteristic functions of sums into products.

## Hypotheses and conventions

The bounded exponential ensures existence for every real random variable.

## Example

For $N(0,1)$, $\varphi(t)=e^{-t^2/2}$.

## Scope and common pitfalls

Moment-generating functions, unlike characteristic functions, need not exist near zero.
