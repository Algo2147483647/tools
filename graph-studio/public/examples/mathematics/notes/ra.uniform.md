# Uniform convergence

Real analysis · Definition · Core

Stable ID: `ra.uniform`

Uniform convergence controls errors simultaneously at every point of the domain.

## Statement

Uniform convergence means $\sup_{x\in X}|f_n(x)-f(x)|\to0$. A uniform limit of continuous functions is continuous.

## Hypotheses and conventions

The same index bound works for every point of the domain.

## Example

$x^n$ on $[0,1]$ converges pointwise but not uniformly.

## Scope and common pitfalls

Differentiation cannot generally be interchanged with a uniform limit without additional derivative control.
