# Closed graph theorem

Functional analysis · Theorem · Core

Stable ID: `fa.closed-graph`

## Statement

An everywhere-defined linear map between Banach spaces is bounded if and only if its graph is closed in the product space.

## Hypotheses and conventions

The map is defined on all of X; X and Y are Banach.

## Example

A closed differential operator may be unbounded when its domain is only a dense proper subspace.

## Scope and common pitfalls

Closedness of a densely defined operator alone does not imply boundedness.

## Proof idea

View the graph as a Banach space and apply the bounded inverse theorem to its projection onto X.

This is a proof strategy; details and intermediate lemmas are in the references.
