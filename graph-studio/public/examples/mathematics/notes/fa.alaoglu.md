# Banach–Alaoglu theorem

Functional analysis · Theorem · Core

Stable ID: `fa.alaoglu`

A dual unit ball is compact for the weak-star topology.

## Statement

The closed unit ball of $X^*$ is compact in the weak-star topology for every normed space X.

## Hypotheses and conventions

Weak-star compactness is not norm compactness.

## Example

If X is separable, this weak-star compact ball is metrizable and hence sequentially compact.

## Scope and common pitfalls

Without metrizability, compactness does not automatically imply sequential compactness.

## Proof idea

Embed the dual ball into a product of compact scalar disks and verify that the functional conditions define a closed subset.

This is a proof strategy; details and intermediate lemmas are in the references.
