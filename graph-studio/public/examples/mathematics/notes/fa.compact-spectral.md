# Compact self-adjoint spectral theorem

Functional analysis · Theorem · Core

Stable ID: `fa.compact-spectral`

## Statement

A compact self-adjoint operator on a Hilbert space has orthogonal finite-dimensional eigenspaces for its nonzero eigenvalues; those eigenvalues can accumulate only at zero. The closed span of these eigenspaces is the orthogonal complement of the kernel.

## Hypotheses and conventions

Self-adjointness and compactness are both assumed.

## Example

A diagonal operator on l2 with real diagonal entries tending to zero is compact and self-adjoint.

## Scope and common pitfalls

A kernel may be infinite-dimensional; zero need not be an eigenvalue when it is a spectral limit point.

## Proof idea

Maximize the quadratic form to obtain an eigenvector, then iterate on orthogonal complements.

This is a proof strategy; details and intermediate lemmas are in the references.
