# Commutative rings, ideals and modules

Algebraic geometry · Definition · Core

Stable ID: `ag.rings`

## Statement

All rings here are commutative with identity and maps preserve identity. An ideal absorbs multiplication by ring elements; a module is the corresponding linear structure with scalars in a ring.

## Hypotheses and conventions

A prime ideal is proper with domain quotient; a maximal ideal has field quotient.

## Example

$(x)$ is prime in $k[x,y]$, whereas $(x,y)$ is maximal.

## Scope and common pitfalls

A module need not have a basis, and prime ideals need not be maximal.
