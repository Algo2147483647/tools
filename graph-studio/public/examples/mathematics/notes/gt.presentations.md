# Free groups and presentations

Group theory · Construction · Core

Stable ID: `gt.presentations`

Generators and relations express a group as a quotient of a free group.

## Statement

The free group F(S) realizes arbitrary maps from S into groups uniquely as homomorphisms. A presentation $\langle S\mid R\rangle$ means $F(S)$ modulo the normal closure of R.

## Hypotheses and conventions

Relations are imposed normally, so all conjugates are also identified with the identity.

## Example

$\langle a\mid a^n=1\rangle$ presents a cyclic group of order n.

## Scope and common pitfalls

Finite presentations do not guarantee an effective general solution of the word problem.
