# Fredholm alternative for I minus compact

Functional analysis · Theorem · Core

Stable ID: `fa.fredholm`

Identity minus a compact operator has equivalent injectivity and surjectivity.

## Statement

For compact K on a Banach space X, $I-K$ is injective if and only if it is surjective. Its kernel is finite-dimensional and its range is closed.

## Hypotheses and conventions

The operator has the particular form identity minus a compact operator.

## Example

A second-kind integral equation has either unique solvability for every right-hand side or a nontrivial homogeneous solution.

## Scope and common pitfalls

For a nontrivial kernel, solvability still requires compatibility conditions; arbitrary bounded operators need not satisfy this alternative.

## Proof idea

Compactness prevents infinite separated sequences in successive kernels or ranges.

This is a proof strategy; details and intermediate lemmas are in the references.
