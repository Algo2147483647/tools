# Topological manifolds

Topology · Definition · Core

Stable ID: `tp.manifolds`

Topological manifolds are locally Euclidean spaces with separation and countability conditions.

## Statement

An n-manifold is a Hausdorff second-countable space locally homeomorphic to $\mathbb R^n$. Manifolds with boundary instead use a closed half-space in their local models.

## Hypotheses and conventions

The boundary convention must be specified; a manifold here has no boundary unless stated.

## Example

Spheres and tori are compact manifolds.

## Scope and common pitfalls

A topological manifold is not automatically supplied with a smooth structure.
