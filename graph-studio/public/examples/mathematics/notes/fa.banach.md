# Banach spaces

Functional analysis · Definition · Core

Stable ID: `fa.banach`

## Statement

A Banach space is a normed real or complex vector space complete for its norm metric.

## Hypotheses and conventions

The scalar field and norm must be specified.

## Example

$L^p(\mu)$ is Banach for $1\le p\le\infty$; $C(K)$ is Banach with the sup norm for compact K.

## Scope and common pitfalls

A linear subspace of a Banach space is complete in the inherited norm exactly when it is closed.
