# Completeness of the real numbers

Real analysis · Axiom · Core

Stable ID: `ra.completeness`

Every nonempty bounded-above real set has a least upper bound.

## Statement

Every nonempty subset of $\mathbb R$ bounded above has a least upper bound. This order completeness supports the convergence theory of real analysis.

## Hypotheses and conventions

Work in the usual ordered field of real numbers.

## Example

The rational numbers fail this property at the cut determined by $x^2<2$.

## Scope and common pitfalls

Completeness is not a consequence of the ordered-field axioms alone.
