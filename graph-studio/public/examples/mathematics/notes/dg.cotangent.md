# Cotangent bundles and tensor fields

Differential geometry · Definition · Core

Stable ID: `dg.cotangent`

Covectors and tensors encode multilinear data intrinsically on a manifold.

## Statement

The cotangent space is $T_p^*M=\operatorname{Hom}(T_pM,\mathbb R)$. Tensor fields are smooth sections of tensor products of tangent and cotangent bundles.

## Hypotheses and conventions

Covariant and contravariant indices transform differently under coordinate changes.

## Example

The differential of a smooth function is a covector field.

## Scope and common pitfalls

A metric identifies vectors and covectors only after that metric is chosen.
