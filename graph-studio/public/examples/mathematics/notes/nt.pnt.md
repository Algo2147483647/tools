# Prime number theorem

Number theory · Theorem · Core

Stable ID: `nt.pnt`

The counting function of primes has a logarithmic asymptotic density.

## Statement

If $\pi(x)$ counts primes at most x, then $\pi(x)\sim x/\log x$ as $x\to\infty$.

## Hypotheses and conventions

The logarithm is natural; no error term is claimed here.

## Example

The average spacing of primes near x has scale log x.

## Scope and common pitfalls

The theorem does not bound each prime gap by a fixed multiple of log x.

## Proof idea

An analytic proof combines nonvanishing of zeta on Re s = 1 with a Tauberian theorem.

This is a proof strategy; details and intermediate lemmas are in the references.
