# Schemes and structure sheaves

Algebraic geometry · Definition · Core

Stable ID: `ag.schemes`

Schemes glue affine spectra with their sheaves of local algebraic functions.

## Statement

A scheme is a locally ringed space locally isomorphic to $(\operatorname{Spec}A,\mathcal O)$, where $\mathcal O(D(f))=A_f$ and the stalk at $\mathfrak p$ is $A_{\mathfrak p}$.

## Hypotheses and conventions

Morphisms of locally ringed spaces induce local homomorphisms on stalks.

## Example

$\operatorname{Spec}k[\epsilon]/(\epsilon^2)$ is a one-point scheme with nontrivial infinitesimal structure.

## Scope and common pitfalls

The underlying topological space does not determine its scheme structure.
