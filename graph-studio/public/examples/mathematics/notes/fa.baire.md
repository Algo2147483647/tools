# Baire category theorem

Functional analysis · Theorem · Core

Stable ID: `fa.baire`

## Statement

A nonempty complete metric space cannot be a countable union of nowhere dense sets. Equivalently, countable intersections of dense open sets are dense.

## Hypotheses and conventions

Completeness is a sufficient hypothesis; locally compact Hausdorff spaces are another important version.

## Example

This underlies the uniform boundedness and open mapping principles.

## Scope and common pitfalls

Meagre and measure-zero are different notions of smallness.

## Proof idea

Choose nested closed balls avoiding successive nowhere dense sets; completeness supplies a common point.

This is a proof strategy; details and intermediate lemmas are in the references.
