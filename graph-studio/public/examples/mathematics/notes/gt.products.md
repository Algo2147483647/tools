# Direct products

Group theory · Construction · Core

Stable ID: `gt.products`

## Statement

The direct product of groups has componentwise multiplication. Internally, commuting normal subgroups with trivial intersection and product G realize G as their direct product.

## Hypotheses and conventions

For the internal two-factor statement, both subgroups are normal and their intersection is trivial.

## Example

$C_2\times C_2$ is not cyclic, whereas $C_2\times C_3\cong C_6$.

## Scope and common pitfalls

Orders alone do not determine a direct-product decomposition.
