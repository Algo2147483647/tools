# Forcing and generic extensions

Set theory and mathematical logic · Research gateway · Advanced gateway

Stable ID: `sl.forcing`

Generic extensions modify a model's sets while preserving its ordinals and ZFC.

## Statement

For a countable transitive model M of ZFC and a forcing notion $\mathbb P\in M$, an externally chosen M-generic filter yields an extension M[G] satisfying ZFC, with the same ordinals.

## Hypotheses and conventions

This introductory semantic setup assumes a countable transitive model; its existence is stronger than merely Con(ZFC). Conditions may be ordered with stronger conditions below weaker ones.

## Example

Cohen forcing adds a new subset of the natural numbers.

## Scope and common pitfalls

A generic filter is generally outside M. Full relative-consistency proofs need not assume that a transitive model actually exists.
