# Covering-space classification

Topology · Theorem · Core

Stable ID: `tp.coverings`

Under local connectivity assumptions, connected coverings correspond to subgroups.

## Statement

For connected, locally path-connected, semilocally simply connected X, connected based coverings correspond to subgroups of $\pi_1(X,x_0)$. Unbased coverings correspond to conjugacy classes of subgroups.

## Hypotheses and conventions

A covering is locally a disjoint union of homeomorphic sheets.

## Example

$z\mapsto z^n$ gives an n-sheeted cover of the circle.

## Scope and common pitfalls

Dropping the local assumptions can break this classification and existence of a universal cover.

## Proof idea

Path lifting gives the subgroup; homotopy classes of paths construct the covering with a prescribed subgroup.

This is a proof strategy; details and intermediate lemmas are in the references.
