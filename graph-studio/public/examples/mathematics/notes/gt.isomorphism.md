# First isomorphism theorem

Group theory · Theorem · Core

Stable ID: `gt.isomorphism`

Quotienting a homomorphism's domain by its kernel recovers its image.

## Statement

For a group homomorphism $\phi:G\to H$, the induced map $G/\ker\phi\to\operatorname{im}\phi$ is an isomorphism.

## Hypotheses and conventions

Surjectivity onto H is unnecessary if the target is restricted to the image.

## Example

The determinant identifies GLn modulo SLn with the nonzero scalars.

## Scope and common pitfalls

The theorem identifies the quotient with the image, not automatically with all of H.

## Proof idea

Send a coset of the kernel to the common image of its representatives and check injectivity and surjectivity.

This is a proof strategy; details and intermediate lemmas are in the references.
