# Serre duality on curves

Algebraic geometry · Theorem · Advanced gateway

Stable ID: `ag.serre-duality`

The canonical bundle pairs first cohomology with a dual space of sections.

## Statement

For a line bundle L on a smooth projective geometrically integral curve C over k, $H^1(C,L)$ is naturally dual to $H^0(C,\omega_C\otimes L^{-1})$.

## Hypotheses and conventions

Finite-dimensionality and the dualizing canonical bundle use properness and smoothness in this version.

## Example

Applying this to the structure sheaf identifies the genus with the dimension of regular differentials.

## Scope and common pitfalls

For singular schemes one needs a dualizing object rather than blindly substituting the sheaf of differentials.

## Proof idea

The trace map on H1 of the canonical bundle turns cup product into a perfect pairing.

This is a proof strategy; details and intermediate lemmas are in the references.
