# Chinese remainder theorem

Number theory · Theorem · Core

Stable ID: `nt.crt`

Coprime congruence conditions combine into one unique residue class.

## Statement

For pairwise coprime positive integers $m_i$, $\mathbb Z/(\prod m_i)\cong\prod_i\mathbb Z/m_i$ as rings via the residue map.

## Hypotheses and conventions

Pairwise coprimality permits arbitrary independent residues.

## Example

$x\equiv2\pmod3$, $x\equiv3\pmod5$ gives $x\equiv8\pmod{15}$.

## Scope and common pitfalls

Without coprimality, residues must satisfy compatibility conditions.

## Proof idea

Bezout coefficients construct idempotents selecting each coordinate.

This is a proof strategy; details and intermediate lemmas are in the references.
