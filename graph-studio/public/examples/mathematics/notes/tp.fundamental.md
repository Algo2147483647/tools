# Fundamental group

Topology · Definition · Core

Stable ID: `tp.fundamental`

Based loops modulo homotopy form a group detecting one-dimensional holes.

## Statement

The group $\pi_1(X,x_0)$ consists of based loops modulo based homotopy, with concatenation as multiplication. Continuous based maps induce homomorphisms.

## Hypotheses and conventions

In a path-connected space basepoint changes give isomorphisms, generally dependent on a chosen path.

## Example

$\pi_1(S^1)\cong\mathbb Z$.

## Scope and common pitfalls

The fundamental group alone does not determine the homotopy type.
