# Levi-Civita connection

Differential geometry · Theorem · Core

Stable ID: `dg.levi-civita`

A Riemannian metric determines a unique torsion-free metric connection.

## Statement

Every Riemannian metric has a unique connection with $\nabla g=0$ and zero torsion: $\nabla_XY-\nabla_YX=[X,Y]$.

## Hypotheses and conventions

The connection is on the tangent bundle and is real-linear in the vector field being differentiated.

## Example

In Cartesian Euclidean coordinates its Christoffel symbols vanish.

## Scope and common pitfalls

Christoffel symbols depend on coordinates and are not tensor components.

## Proof idea

The Koszul formula both determines the connection uniquely and constructs it.

This is a proof strategy; details and intermediate lemmas are in the references.
