# Number fields, norm and trace

Number theory · Definition · Core

Stable ID: `nt.number-fields`

Finite rational extensions carry field norms, traces and complex embeddings.

## Statement

A number field is a finite extension K of $\mathbb Q$. The determinant and trace of multiplication by a define $N_{K/\mathbb Q}(a)$ and $\operatorname{Tr}_{K/\mathbb Q}(a)$.

## Hypotheses and conventions

Characteristic zero ensures separability; the degree counts complex embeddings.

## Example

In $\mathbb Q(\sqrt d)$, $N(a+b\sqrt d)=a^2-db^2$.

## Scope and common pitfalls

Field norm is multiplicative, not a metric-space norm.
