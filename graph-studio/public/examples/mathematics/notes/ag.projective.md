# Projective space and Proj

Algebraic geometry · Construction · Core

Stable ID: `ag.projective`

Proj constructs projective geometry from graded commutative algebra.

## Statement

Projective n-space over A is $\operatorname{Proj}A[x_0,\ldots,x_n]$. For a nonnegatively graded ring S, Proj uses homogeneous prime ideals not containing the irrelevant ideal $S_+$.

## Hypotheses and conventions

Its standard affine charts use degree-zero parts of homogeneous localizations.

## Example

Homogeneous equations define projective subschemes, including a smooth plane cubic.

## Scope and common pitfalls

Homogeneous coordinates are defined up to simultaneous nonzero scaling over a field.
