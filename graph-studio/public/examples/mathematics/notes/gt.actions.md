# Group actions

Group theory · Definition · Core

Stable ID: `gt.actions`

Actions represent group elements as compatible permutations of a set.

## Statement

An action of G on X is a homomorphism $G\to\operatorname{Sym}(X)$. Orbits partition X and stabilizers are subgroups.

## Hypotheses and conventions

Specify left or right actions and use one convention consistently.

## Example

Conjugation is an action of a group on itself; its stabilizers are centralizers.

## Scope and common pitfalls

Faithful and transitive describe different properties.
