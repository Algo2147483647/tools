# Unbounded self-adjoint operators

Functional analysis · Definition · Advanced gateway

Stable ID: `fa.unbounded`

Self-adjointness of an unbounded operator includes equality of operator domains.

## Statement

A densely defined operator A is self-adjoint when $A=A^*$ including equality of domains. Such operators have real spectrum and a spectral calculus respecting domains.

## Hypotheses and conventions

Specify the dense domain and adjoint domain, not just a formal expression.

## Example

Different boundary conditions for a differential expression can produce different self-adjoint operators.

## Scope and common pitfalls

A symmetric operator need not be self-adjoint or essentially self-adjoint.
