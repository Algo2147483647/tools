# Sobolev spaces

Real analysis · Definition · Advanced gateway

Stable ID: `ra.sobolev`

Sobolev spaces control integrability of a function and its weak derivatives.

## Statement

For integer $k\ge0$ and $1\le p\le\infty$, $W^{k,p}(U)$ consists of Lp functions whose distributional derivatives of order at most k also lie in Lp.

## Hypotheses and conventions

Representatives are identified almost everywhere; the domain is open.

## Example

$W^{1,2}$ is the natural energy space for many elliptic equations.

## Scope and common pitfalls

Boundary traces and compact embeddings require extra domain and exponent assumptions.
