# Algebraic integers

Number theory · Definition · Core

Stable ID: `nt.integers`

Monic integer equations single out the integral arithmetic inside a number field.

## Statement

An element integral over $\mathbb Z$ satisfies a monic polynomial with integer coefficients. The integers of K form a ring $\mathcal O_K$, free of rank $[K:\mathbb Q]$ over $\mathbb Z$.

## Hypotheses and conventions

K is a number field; a rational algebraic integer is an integer.

## Example

$\mathcal O_{\mathbb Q(\sqrt5)}=\mathbb Z[(1+\sqrt5)/2]$.

## Scope and common pitfalls

A field generator need not generate its entire ring of integers.
