# Continuum hypothesis and independence

Set theory and mathematical logic · Theorem · Core

Stable ID: `sl.ch`

The continuum hypothesis is independent of ZFC, assuming ZFC is consistent.

## Statement

CH asserts $2^{\aleph_0}=\aleph_1$. If ZFC is consistent, both ZFC + CH and ZFC + not-CH are consistent. Consequently ZFC proves neither CH nor its negation under that consistency assumption.

## Hypotheses and conventions

This is relative consistency, not a proof that ZFC is consistent.

## Example

The constructible universe supplies CH; forcing supplies models where CH fails.

## Scope and common pitfalls

Independence is always relative to a specified axiom system.

## Proof idea

Gödel's constructible universe gives one direction; Cohen forcing gives the other via a metatheoretic consistency argument.

This is a proof strategy; details and intermediate lemmas are in the references.
