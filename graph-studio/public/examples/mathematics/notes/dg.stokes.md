# Stokes' theorem

Differential geometry · Theorem · Core

Stable ID: `dg.stokes`

Integrating an exterior derivative equals integrating the form over the oriented boundary.

## Statement

For an oriented smooth n-manifold M with boundary and a compactly supported smooth (n-1)-form omega, $\int_M d\omega=\int_{\partial M}\omega$.

## Hypotheses and conventions

The boundary orientation uses the outward-normal-first convention; compact support controls behavior at infinity.

## Example

The fundamental theorem of calculus and the classical divergence theorem are instances.

## Scope and common pitfalls

Changing the boundary orientation changes the sign.

## Proof idea

Prove the formula in half-space coordinates and combine the local identities with a partition of unity.

This is a proof strategy; details and intermediate lemmas are in the references.
