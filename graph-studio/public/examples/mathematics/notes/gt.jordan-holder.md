# Jordan–Hölder theorem

Group theory · Theorem · Core

Stable ID: `gt.jordan-holder`

## Statement

Any two composition series of a finite group have the same simple factors up to isomorphism and permutation.

## Hypotheses and conventions

A composition series has simple successive quotients and strictly descending subgroups.

## Example

Composition factors are structural invariants even when a group has different composition series.

## Scope and common pitfalls

The factors do not determine the group: extension data remain.

## Proof idea

Refine and compare series using the isomorphism theorems and a refinement argument.

This is a proof strategy; details and intermediate lemmas are in the references.
