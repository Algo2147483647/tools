# Sheaves and stalks

Algebraic geometry · Definition · Core

Stable ID: `ag.sheaves`

## Statement

A sheaf assigns data to open sets with restriction maps, uniqueness of gluing and existence of gluing for compatible local sections. A stalk records germs near one point.

## Hypotheses and conventions

Both gluing axioms distinguish a sheaf from a general presheaf.

## Example

Smooth functions form a sheaf; constant sections require sheafification to capture locally constant functions.

## Scope and common pitfalls

Exactness of sheaves is checked on stalks, while taking global sections need not preserve surjections.
