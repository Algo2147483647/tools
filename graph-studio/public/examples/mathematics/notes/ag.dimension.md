# Dimension and Noether normalization

Algebraic geometry · Theorem · Core

Stable ID: `ag.dimension`

## Statement

A finitely generated integral k-algebra A is finite over a polynomial subalgebra in d algebraically independent elements. Its Krull dimension equals d and equals the transcendence degree of its fraction field over k.

## Hypotheses and conventions

A is a domain of finite type over a field.

## Example

An irreducible plane curve has dimension one.

## Scope and common pitfalls

Embedding dimension, tangent-space dimension and Krull dimension need not coincide at singular points.

## Proof idea

Choose a change of generators making one algebraic relation monic, then induct on the number of generators.

This is a proof strategy; details and intermediate lemmas are in the references.
