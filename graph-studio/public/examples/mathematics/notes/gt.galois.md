# Finite Galois correspondence

Group theory · Theorem · Advanced gateway

Stable ID: `gt.galois`

Finite Galois extensions reverse the inclusions between subfields and subgroups.

## Statement

For a finite Galois extension L/K, intermediate fields E correspond inclusion-reversingly to subgroups of $\operatorname{Gal}(L/K)$. Normal subgroups correspond to extensions E/K that are Galois.

## Hypotheses and conventions

The extension must be finite, normal and separable.

## Example

The splitting field of $x^3-2$ over $\mathbb Q$ has group $S_3$.

## Scope and common pitfalls

An arbitrary finite field extension does not satisfy this subgroup–subfield correspondence in the same form.

## Proof idea

Use fixed fields and independence of field embeddings to identify subgroup indices with field degrees.

This is a proof strategy; details and intermediate lemmas are in the references.
