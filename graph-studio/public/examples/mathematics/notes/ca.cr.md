# Cauchy–Riemann equations

Complex analysis · Theorem · Core

Stable ID: `ca.cr`

The real differential of a holomorphic function is complex linear.

## Statement

For $f=u+iv$ with real $C^1$ components, holomorphy is equivalent to $u_x=v_y$ and $u_y=-v_x$. Equivalently, $\partial_{\bar z}f=0$.

## Hypotheses and conventions

The sufficient condition here assumes continuous first partial derivatives on an open set.

## Example

For $e^{x+iy}$, the equations hold everywhere.

## Scope and common pitfalls

Pointwise equations without regularity are not the stated sufficient criterion.

## Proof idea

Identify the real differential with multiplication by a complex number.

This is a proof strategy; details and intermediate lemmas are in the references.
