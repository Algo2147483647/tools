# Separation axioms

Topology · Definition · Core

Stable ID: `tp.separation`

## Statement

A Hausdorff space separates distinct points by disjoint open neighborhoods. A regular T1 space separates a point from a disjoint closed set; a normal T1 space separates disjoint closed sets.

## Hypotheses and conventions

The T1 condition is included explicitly in the regular and normal conventions here.

## Example

Metric spaces are normal and Hausdorff.

## Scope and common pitfalls

Separation axioms are distinct from countability axioms.
