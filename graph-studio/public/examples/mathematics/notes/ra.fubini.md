# Tonelli and Fubini

Real analysis · Theorem · Core

Stable ID: `ra.fubini`

## Statement

On sigma-finite measure spaces, nonnegative measurable functions have equal iterated and product integrals, possibly infinite. For absolutely integrable functions, the iterated integrals agree and sections are integrable almost everywhere.

## Hypotheses and conventions

Use the product sigma-algebra; completed-measure formulations require the corresponding almost-everywhere interpretation.

## Example

Nonnegative double series may be rearranged freely; absolutely summable signed series also may be rearranged.

## Scope and common pitfalls

Conditional convergence does not justify interchanging two integrals.

## Proof idea

Start with measurable rectangles, extend to simple functions, and then use monotone convergence; handle signed functions by absolute integrability.

This is a proof strategy; details and intermediate lemmas are in the references.
