# Localization and local rings

Algebraic geometry · Construction · Core

Stable ID: `ag.localization`

Localization inverts selected ring elements and focuses algebra near a prime ideal.

## Statement

For a multiplicative subset S, $S^{-1}A$ universally makes elements of S invertible. Localizing at $S=A\setminus\mathfrak p$ gives the local ring $A_{\mathfrak p}$ with maximal ideal $\mathfrak p A_{\mathfrak p}$.

## Hypotheses and conventions

A local ring has a unique maximal ideal; localization may be the zero ring if zero lies in S.

## Example

$\mathbb Z_{(p)}$ consists of rationals whose denominators are not divisible by p.

## Scope and common pitfalls

Localization and quotient impose different operations: inversion versus setting elements equal to zero.
