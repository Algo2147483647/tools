# Riemann surfaces

Complex analysis · Definition · Advanced gateway

Stable ID: `ca.riemann-surfaces`

## Statement

A Riemann surface is a connected, Hausdorff, second-countable complex manifold of complex dimension one, with holomorphic coordinate transitions.

## Hypotheses and conventions

The topology and complex atlas are part of the structure.

## Example

The Riemann sphere and complex tori $\mathbb C/\Lambda$ are compact examples.

## Scope and common pitfalls

A topological surface may carry many inequivalent conformal structures; compact does not mean biholomorphic to the sphere.
