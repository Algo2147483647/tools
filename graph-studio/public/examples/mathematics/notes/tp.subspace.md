# Subspace, product and quotient topologies

Topology · Construction · Core

Stable ID: `tp.subspace`

## Statement

Subspaces inherit intersections with ambient open sets. Product topology is generated by finite-coordinate restrictions. A quotient map declares a set open exactly when its preimage is open.

## Hypotheses and conventions

The infinite product topology differs from the box topology.

## Example

Identifying the endpoints of a closed interval produces a circle.

## Scope and common pitfalls

Quotients of Hausdorff spaces need not be Hausdorff.
