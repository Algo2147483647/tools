# Cosets and Lagrange's theorem

Group theory · Theorem · Core

Stable ID: `gt.lagrange`

Cosets partition a finite group into equal-sized pieces.

## Statement

The left cosets of H partition G. If G is finite, $|G|=[G:H]|H|$, so subgroup orders and element orders divide $|G|$.

## Hypotheses and conventions

The divisibility statement is for finite groups.

## Example

A group of prime order is cyclic.

## Scope and common pitfalls

A divisor of the group order need not be the order of a subgroup.

## Proof idea

Each coset is in bijection with H by translation, and distinct cosets are disjoint.

This is a proof strategy; details and intermediate lemmas are in the references.
