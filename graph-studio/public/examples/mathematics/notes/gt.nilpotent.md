# Central series and nilpotent groups

Group theory · Definition · Core

Stable ID: `gt.nilpotent`

Nilpotence requires termination of the lower central series.

## Statement

A group is nilpotent when its lower central series $\gamma_1(G)=G$, $\gamma_{i+1}(G)=[G,\gamma_i(G)]$ reaches the identity.

## Hypotheses and conventions

This is finite-step nilpotence, not local nilpotence.

## Example

Every finite p-group is nilpotent; S3 is solvable but not nilpotent.

## Scope and common pitfalls

Nilpotence implies solvability, but the converse fails.
