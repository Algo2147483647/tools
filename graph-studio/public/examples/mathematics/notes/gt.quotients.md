# Normal subgroups and quotients

Group theory · Construction · Core

Stable ID: `gt.quotients`

Normality makes multiplication on cosets independent of representatives.

## Statement

A subgroup N is normal when $gNg^{-1}=N$ for every g. Then multiplication of cosets makes $G/N$ a group, and N is the kernel of the quotient map.

## Hypotheses and conventions

Normality ensures that coset multiplication is independent of representatives.

## Example

$S_n/A_n$ has order two for $n\ge2$.

## Scope and common pitfalls

A coset set by a nonnormal subgroup is generally not a quotient group.
