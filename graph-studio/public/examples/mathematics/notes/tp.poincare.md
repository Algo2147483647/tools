# Poincaré duality

Topology · Theorem · Advanced gateway

Stable ID: `tp.poincare`

Orientation pairs complementary homology and cohomology degrees on a closed manifold.

## Statement

For a closed connected oriented n-manifold M, cap product with its fundamental class gives $H^k(M;\mathbb Z)\cong H_{n-k}(M;\mathbb Z)$.

## Hypotheses and conventions

Closed means compact without boundary; nonorientable manifolds require suitable coefficients or local systems.

## Example

On an oriented closed surface, first cohomology pairs nondegenerately by intersection.

## Scope and common pitfalls

For noncompact manifolds or manifolds with boundary the duality statement needs compact supports or relative groups.

## Proof idea

Patch local orientation classes using relative homology and Mayer–Vietoris.

This is a proof strategy; details and intermediate lemmas are in the references.
