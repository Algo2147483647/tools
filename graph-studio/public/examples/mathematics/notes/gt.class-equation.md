# Class equation and finite p-groups

Group theory · Theorem · Core

Stable ID: `gt.class-equation`

Conjugacy-class counting forces nontrivial centers in finite prime-power groups.

## Statement

A finite group decomposes as $|G|=|Z(G)|+\sum [G:C_G(x_i)]$, summing over noncentral conjugacy classes. A nontrivial finite p-group has nontrivial center.

## Hypotheses and conventions

The p-group conclusion assumes the group order is a positive power of a prime.

## Example

Every group of order p squared is abelian.

## Scope and common pitfalls

A nontrivial center does not by itself make a group abelian.

## Proof idea

Apply orbit–stabilizer to conjugation and reduce the class equation modulo p.

This is a proof strategy; details and intermediate lemmas are in the references.
