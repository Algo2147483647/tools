# Noetherian rings and Hilbert basis theorem

Algebraic geometry · Theorem · Core

Stable ID: `ag.noetherian`

## Statement

A ring is Noetherian exactly when every ideal is finitely generated. If A is Noetherian, its polynomial ring in finitely many variables is Noetherian.

## Hypotheses and conventions

The equivalence uses the ascending chain condition on ideals.

## Example

Every finitely generated algebra over a field is Noetherian.

## Scope and common pitfalls

A polynomial ring in infinitely many variables over a field is not Noetherian.

## Proof idea

Control leading coefficients in an ascending chain of polynomial ideals and reduce degrees inductively.

This is a proof strategy; details and intermediate lemmas are in the references.
