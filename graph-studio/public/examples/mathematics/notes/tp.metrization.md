# Urysohn metrization theorem

Topology · Theorem · Core

Stable ID: `tp.metrization`

## Statement

Every second-countable regular T1 space is metrizable.

## Hypotheses and conventions

Second countability means a countable base, not merely a countable dense subset.

## Example

The theorem supplies compatible metrics for many abstractly defined spaces.

## Scope and common pitfalls

A general regular separable space need not be metrizable.

## Proof idea

Construct countably many continuous functions separating points and embed the space in a countable cube.

This is a proof strategy; details and intermediate lemmas are in the references.
