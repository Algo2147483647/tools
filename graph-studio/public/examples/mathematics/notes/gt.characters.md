# Characters and orthogonality

Group theory · Theorem · Advanced gateway

Stable ID: `gt.characters`

Irreducible complex characters form an orthonormal basis of class functions.

## Statement

For finite G over $\mathbb C$, irreducible characters form an orthonormal basis of class functions for $\langle\chi,\psi\rangle=|G|^{-1}\sum_g\chi(g)\overline{\psi(g)}$.

## Hypotheses and conventions

Characters are traces of finite-dimensional representations; the inner product is over all group elements.

## Example

Character inner products compute multiplicities in a representation.

## Scope and common pitfalls

This ordinary-character statement does not transfer unchanged to modular representation theory.

## Proof idea

Average intertwining maps and apply Schur's lemma.

This is a proof strategy; details and intermediate lemmas are in the references.
