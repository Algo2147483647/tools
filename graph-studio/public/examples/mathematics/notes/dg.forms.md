# Differential forms and exterior derivative

Differential geometry · Definition · Core

Stable ID: `dg.forms`

Alternating tensor fields carry a coordinate-independent exterior derivative.

## Statement

A k-form is a smooth alternating covariant k-tensor. The exterior derivative satisfies $d^2=0$ and $d(\alpha\wedge\beta)=d\alpha\wedge\beta+(-1)^k\alpha\wedge d\beta$.

## Hypotheses and conventions

Here alpha has degree k; pullback commutes with exterior derivative.

## Example

On the punctured plane, $(-y\,dx+x\,dy)/(x^2+y^2)$ is closed but not exact.

## Scope and common pitfalls

Closed forms need not be globally exact.
