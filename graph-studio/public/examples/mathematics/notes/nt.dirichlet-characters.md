# Dirichlet characters and L-functions

Number theory · Definition · Core

Stable ID: `nt.dirichlet-characters`

Multiplicative characters separate residue classes through their L-functions.

## Statement

A character modulo q is a character of $(\mathbb Z/q\mathbb Z)^\times$, extended by zero on nonunits. For $\operatorname{Re}s>1$, $L(s,\chi)=\sum_{n\ge1}\chi(n)n^{-s}$.

## Hypotheses and conventions

Distinguish primitive characters from characters induced from smaller moduli.

## Example

Character orthogonality isolates reduced residue classes.

## Scope and common pitfalls

The principal character has Euler factors removed at primes dividing q.
