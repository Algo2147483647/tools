# Continuous dual and bidual

Functional analysis · Definition · Core

Stable ID: `fa.dual`

The continuous dual records bounded linear measurements of a normed space.

## Statement

The continuous dual $X^*$ consists of bounded linear scalar-valued functionals. The canonical map $J:X\to X^{**}$ is given by $Jx(\ell)=\ell(x)$ and is isometric.

## Hypotheses and conventions

Use the continuous dual rather than the algebraic dual.

## Example

For $1<p<\infty$ on a sigma-finite measure space, $(L^p)^*$ is identified with $L^q$ by integration.

## Scope and common pitfalls

The full dual of $L^\infty$ generally exceeds $L^1$.
