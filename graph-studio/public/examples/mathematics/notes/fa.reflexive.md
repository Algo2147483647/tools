# Reflexive spaces

Functional analysis · Theorem · Core

Stable ID: `fa.reflexive`

Reflexivity identifies a Banach space with its bidual and gives weak ball compactness.

## Statement

A Banach space X is reflexive when $J:X\to X^{**}$ is onto. This is equivalent to weak compactness of its closed unit ball.

## Hypotheses and conventions

Weak topology is the topology induced by the continuous dual.

## Example

Hilbert spaces and $L^p$ for $1<p<\infty$ are reflexive.

## Scope and common pitfalls

$L^1([0,1])$ and $L^\infty([0,1])$ with Lebesgue measure are not reflexive.

## Proof idea

For reflexive spaces identify the ball with a weak-star compact bidual ball; the converse uses separation and density of the canonical image.

This is a proof strategy; details and intermediate lemmas are in the references.
