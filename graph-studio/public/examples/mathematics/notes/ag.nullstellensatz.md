# Hilbert Nullstellensatz

Algebraic geometry · Theorem · Core

Stable ID: `ag.nullstellensatz`

Over an algebraically closed field, polynomial zero sets correspond to radical ideals.

## Statement

Over an algebraically closed field k, maximal ideals of $k[x_1,\ldots,x_n]$ are point ideals and $I(V(I))=\sqrt I$.

## Hypotheses and conventions

The field must be algebraically closed; the number of variables is finite.

## Example

Over $\mathbb C$, the ideal $(x^2)$ and its radical $(x)$ have the same zero set.

## Scope and common pitfalls

Point sets alone do not retain nilpotent structure.

## Proof idea

Zariski's lemma proves the maximal-ideal statement; the Rabinowitsch trick deduces the radical statement.

This is a proof strategy; details and intermediate lemmas are in the references.
