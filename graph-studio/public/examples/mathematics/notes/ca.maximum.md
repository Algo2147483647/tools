# Maximum modulus principle

Complex analysis · Theorem · Core

Stable ID: `ca.maximum`

## Statement

A nonconstant holomorphic function on a connected domain cannot have a local interior maximum of its modulus.

## Hypotheses and conventions

The maximized quantity is the modulus, not an ordering of complex values.

## Example

For a function continuous on a closed disk and holomorphic inside, its maximum modulus is attained on the boundary.

## Scope and common pitfalls

The boundary conclusion requires compactness and continuous extension to the boundary.

## Proof idea

Use the mean-value identity and its equality case, or the local power-series expansion.

This is a proof strategy; details and intermediate lemmas are in the references.
