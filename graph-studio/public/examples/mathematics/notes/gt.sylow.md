# Sylow theorems

Group theory · Theorem · Core

Stable ID: `gt.sylow`

Prime-power subgroups obey existence, conjugacy and counting constraints.

## Statement

If $|G|=p^a m$ with $p\nmid m$, subgroups of order $p^a$ exist, every p-subgroup is contained in one, all such maximal p-subgroups are conjugate, and their number divides m and is $1\bmod p$.

## Hypotheses and conventions

G is finite and p is prime.

## Example

A unique Sylow p-subgroup is normal.

## Scope and common pitfalls

The congruence and divisibility constraints may not uniquely determine the number of Sylow subgroups.

## Proof idea

Use group actions on subsets or cosets to force fixed points and conjugacy.

This is a proof strategy; details and intermediate lemmas are in the references.
