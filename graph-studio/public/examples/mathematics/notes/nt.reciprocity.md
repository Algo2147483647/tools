# Quadratic reciprocity

Number theory · Theorem · Core

Stable ID: `nt.reciprocity`

Quadratic reciprocity relates square residues modulo two odd primes.

## Statement

For distinct odd primes p,q, $(\frac pq)(\frac qp)=(-1)^{(p-1)(q-1)/4}$. Also $(\frac{-1}p)=(-1)^{(p-1)/2}$ and $(\frac2p)=(-1)^{(p^2-1)/8}$.

## Hypotheses and conventions

The Legendre symbol is 1 for a nonzero square, -1 for a nonsquare, and 0 for a multiple of p.

## Example

$(\frac3{11})=1$.

## Scope and common pitfalls

A Jacobi symbol of one does not certify a square modulo a composite integer.

## Proof idea

Gauss's lemma turns the symbols into parity counts of lattice points.

This is a proof strategy; details and intermediate lemmas are in the references.
