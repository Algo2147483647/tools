# Proper and projective morphisms

Algebraic geometry · Definition · Core

Stable ID: `ag.proper`

## Statement

A proper morphism is separated, of finite type and universally closed. A projective morphism factors as a closed immersion into a finite-dimensional projective space over the base and is proper.

## Hypotheses and conventions

Universal closedness means closedness after every base change.

## Example

Projective space is proper over its ground field; affine space of positive dimension is not.

## Scope and common pitfalls

Proper need not mean projective, and topological compactness is not the definition.
