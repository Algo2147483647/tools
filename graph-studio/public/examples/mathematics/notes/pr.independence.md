# Independence and product laws

Probability theory · Definition · Core

Stable ID: `pr.independence`

## Statement

Sigma-algebras are independent when probabilities of intersections factor for every finite subfamily. Variables are independent when their generated sigma-algebras are independent.

## Hypotheses and conventions

Mutual independence requires more than pairwise independence.

## Example

Two independent fair bits and their sum modulo two are pairwise independent but not mutually independent.

## Scope and common pitfalls

Zero covariance alone does not imply independence.
