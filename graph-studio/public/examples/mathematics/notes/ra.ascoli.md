# Arzelà–Ascoli theorem

Real analysis · Theorem · Core

Stable ID: `ra.ascoli`

## Statement

On a compact metric space, a family of real or complex continuous functions is relatively compact in the uniform norm exactly when it is equicontinuous and pointwise bounded.

## Hypotheses and conventions

This statement is for scalar-valued functions on a compact metric domain.

## Example

Uniformly bounded functions with a common Lipschitz constant satisfy the criterion.

## Scope and common pitfalls

A bounded subset of a function space is not automatically relatively compact.

## Proof idea

Approximate functions on finite nets and extract a diagonal subsequence; equicontinuity upgrades pointwise control to uniform control.

This is a proof strategy; details and intermediate lemmas are in the references.
