# Identity theorem

Complex analysis · Theorem · Core

Stable ID: `ca.identity`

## Statement

Two holomorphic functions on a connected domain coincide if their agreement set has an accumulation point in that domain.

## Hypotheses and conventions

The accumulation point is interior, and the domain is connected.

## Example

A holomorphic function vanishing on a real interval inside its domain is identically zero.

## Scope and common pitfalls

Zeros may accumulate at a boundary point without forcing global vanishing.

## Proof idea

The first nonzero Taylor coefficient makes a zero isolated; propagate equality across the connected domain.

This is a proof strategy; details and intermediate lemmas are in the references.
