# Lie groups and Lie algebras

Differential geometry · Definition · Core

Stable ID: `dg.lie`

## Statement

A Lie group is a smooth manifold with smooth multiplication and inverse. Left-invariant vector fields identify its tangent space at the identity with a Lie algebra.

## Hypotheses and conventions

The bracket is inherited from vector-field commutators; finite-dimensional real Lie groups are the default here.

## Example

For matrix groups the Lie bracket is the matrix commutator.

## Scope and common pitfalls

The Lie algebra captures local structure but need not distinguish globally different Lie groups.
