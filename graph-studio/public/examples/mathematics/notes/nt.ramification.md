# Splitting and ramification

Number theory · Theorem · Core

Stable ID: `nt.ramification`

Prime ideals describe splitting, residue degrees and ramification of rational primes.

## Statement

In $\mathcal O_K$, $p\mathcal O_K=\prod_i\mathfrak p_i^{e_i}$ with residue degrees $f_i$ and $\sum_i e_if_i=[K:\mathbb Q]$. A rational prime ramifies exactly when it divides $d_K$.

## Hypotheses and conventions

Use the full ring of integers of a number field.

## Example

In $\mathbb Z[i]$, $2=-i(1+i)^2$.

## Scope and common pitfalls

Factoring a defining polynomial can mislead at primes dividing the index of the chosen order.

## Proof idea

Compare residue-field dimensions and detect ramification through the trace pairing.

This is a proof strategy; details and intermediate lemmas are in the references.
