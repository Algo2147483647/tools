# Probability spaces and random variables

Probability theory · Definition · Core

Stable ID: `pr.space`

Random variables are measurable maps whose laws are probability measures.

## Statement

A probability space $(\Omega,\mathcal F,\mathbb P)$ is a measure space of mass one. A random variable is a measurable map to a measurable state space; its law is the pushforward measure.

## Hypotheses and conventions

Events must belong to the chosen sigma-algebra.

## Example

Real random variables have laws on the Borel sets of the real line.

## Scope and common pitfalls

A random variable and its law are different objects.
