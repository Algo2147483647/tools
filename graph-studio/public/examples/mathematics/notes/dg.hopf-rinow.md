# Hopf–Rinow theorem

Differential geometry · Theorem · Core

Stable ID: `dg.hopf-rinow`

## Statement

For a connected finite-dimensional Riemannian manifold without boundary, metric completeness is equivalent to geodesic completeness and to compactness of closed bounded subsets. Completeness also ensures a minimizing geodesic between any two points.

## Hypotheses and conventions

The metric is the Riemannian length metric; finite dimension is essential.

## Example

A compact Riemannian manifold without boundary is complete.

## Scope and common pitfalls

General infinite-dimensional Hilbert manifolds do not satisfy this entire equivalence.

## Proof idea

Extend radial minimizing geodesics using compactness of tangent spheres and the metric completion argument.

This is a proof strategy; details and intermediate lemmas are in the references.
