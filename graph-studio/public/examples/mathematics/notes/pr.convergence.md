# Modes of convergence

Probability theory · Definition · Core

Stable ID: `pr.convergence`

Almost-sure, probability, distribution and mean convergence are distinct notions.

## Statement

Almost-sure convergence implies convergence in probability, which implies convergence in distribution. $L^p$ convergence for $p\ge1$ implies convergence in probability.

## Hypotheses and conventions

Almost-sure and probability convergence compare variables on one probability space; convergence in law compares distributions.

## Example

Rare tall spikes may converge in probability without converging in mean.

## Scope and common pitfalls

Convergence in law generally does not imply convergence in probability.
