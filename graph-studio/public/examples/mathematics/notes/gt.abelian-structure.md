# Finitely generated abelian groups

Group theory · Theorem · Core

Stable ID: `gt.abelian-structure`

A finitely generated abelian group splits into free and finite cyclic factors.

## Statement

Every finitely generated abelian group is $\mathbb Z^r\oplus\mathbb Z/d_1\oplus\cdots\oplus\mathbb Z/d_k$ with $1<d_1\mid\cdots\mid d_k$; the rank and invariant factors are unique.

## Hypotheses and conventions

Finite generation is essential to this classification.

## Example

$\mathbb Z\oplus\mathbb Z/12$ combines a free part and torsion.

## Scope and common pitfalls

The additive rational group is not covered by this finite-generation theorem.

## Proof idea

Apply Smith normal form to a finite relation matrix over the integers.

This is a proof strategy; details and intermediate lemmas are in the references.
