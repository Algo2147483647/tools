# Signed measures and decomposition

Real analysis · Theorem · Core

Stable ID: `ra.signed`

Finite signed measures split uniquely into mutually singular positive parts.

## Statement

A finite signed measure has a Hahn decomposition and a unique Jordan decomposition $\nu=\nu^+-\nu^-$ into mutually singular finite positive measures. Its total variation is $|\nu|=\nu^++\nu^-$.

## Hypotheses and conventions

This entry restricts to finite signed measures to avoid ambiguous infinite differences.

## Example

An integrable density splits into its positive and negative parts.

## Scope and common pitfalls

The Hahn sets are not unique as sets; the Jordan component measures are unique.

## Proof idea

Construct positive and negative measurable regions, then restrict the measure to obtain its canonical components.

This is a proof strategy; details and intermediate lemmas are in the references.
