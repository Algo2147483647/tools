# Kolmogorov extension theorem

Probability theory · Theorem · Core

Stable ID: `pr.extension`

## Statement

A consistent family of finite-dimensional probability laws on products of standard Borel state spaces determines a unique probability measure on the cylinder sigma-algebra of the full product.

## Hypotheses and conventions

Marginalization and coordinate permutations preserve the prescribed laws.

## Example

Consistent Gaussian laws of covariance min(s,t) specify Brownian finite-dimensional distributions.

## Scope and common pitfalls

Extension alone does not give continuous sample paths.

## Proof idea

Define cylinder probabilities and use the state-space regularity to establish the countable additivity needed for extension.

This is a proof strategy; details and intermediate lemmas are in the references.
