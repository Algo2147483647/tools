# Compact operators

Functional analysis · Definition · Core

Stable ID: `fa.compact`

## Statement

A bounded operator is compact if it sends the unit ball to a relatively norm-compact set.

## Hypotheses and conventions

The domain and codomain are normed; the standard operator theory below uses Banach spaces.

## Example

Finite-rank operators are compact, and their operator-norm limits into a Banach space are compact.

## Scope and common pitfalls

The identity on an infinite-dimensional Banach space is not compact.
