# Group theory

Symmetry through actions, quotient structures, finite-group structure and representation theory.

## Scope

Selected advanced-undergraduate and beginning-graduate core with advanced gateways. Entries give precise scoped statements and proof ideas, not full proofs or exhaustive courses.

## Assumed background

Sets, maps, equivalence relations and finite-dimensional linear algebra; field basics for Galois theory.

## Learning outcomes

- Move between internal group structure, quotient maps and actions.
- Use Sylow arithmetic and conjugation to constrain finite groups.
- Relate representations and Galois groups to their underlying algebraic hypotheses.

## Problems for active study

1. Classify groups of order 15 using Sylow's theorems and commuting normal Sylow subgroups.
2. Construct the character table of S3 and decompose its permutation representation on three letters.
