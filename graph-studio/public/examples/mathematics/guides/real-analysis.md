# Real analysis

Limits, measure, integration, convergence and the function spaces underlying modern analysis.

## Scope

Selected advanced-undergraduate and beginning-graduate core with advanced gateways. Entries give precise scoped statements and proof ideas, not full proofs or exhaustive courses.

## Assumed background

Proof-based calculus, sequences, elementary linear algebra and set operations.

## Learning outcomes

- Choose the correct convergence theorem before exchanging limits and integrals.
- Distinguish pointwise, almost-everywhere, uniform and norm convergence.
- Use measures, densities and weak derivatives as separate mathematical objects.

## Problems for active study

1. For f_n(x)=n times the indicator of (0,1/n) on (0,1), compute the pointwise limit and the integrals. Identify precisely which dominated-convergence hypothesis fails.
2. Prove that Lq is contained in Lp on a finite measure space when 1 <= p < q. Construct counterexamples to both inclusions on the real line with Lebesgue measure.
