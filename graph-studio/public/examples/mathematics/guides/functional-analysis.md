# Functional analysis

Banach and Hilbert spaces, duality, operator theory and variational methods.

## Scope

Selected advanced-undergraduate and beginning-graduate core with advanced gateways. Entries give precise scoped statements and proof ideas, not full proofs or exhaustive courses.

## Assumed background

Linear algebra, metric completeness, Lebesgue integration, Lp spaces and basic topology.

## Learning outcomes

- Track completeness and domain assumptions in the central Banach-space theorems.
- Distinguish weak, weak-star and norm topology.
- Choose the spectral theorem appropriate to the operator and formulate variational problems correctly.

## Problems for active study

1. Prove that an orthonormal sequence converges weakly to zero but has no norm-convergent subsequence.
2. On l2, classify the boundedness, compactness, spectrum and eigenvalues of the diagonal operator with diagonal entries 1/n. Explain the role of zero.
