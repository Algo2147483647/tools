# Complex analysis

Holomorphic rigidity, contour methods, analytic continuation and the geometry of Riemann surfaces.

## Scope

Selected advanced-undergraduate and beginning-graduate core with advanced gateways. Entries give precise scoped statements and proof ideas, not full proofs or exhaustive courses.

## Assumed background

Real multivariable calculus, metric limits, elementary topology and line integration.

## Learning outcomes

- Use Cauchy theory to derive local analyticity and rigidity.
- Choose contour, residue and zero-counting arguments with the correct domain hypotheses.
- Separate local continuation from global single-valuedness.

## Problems for active study

1. Compute the integral of 1/(1+x^4) over the real line by an upper half-plane contour. Justify the vanishing arc estimate.
2. Explain why a holomorphic logarithm exists on a simply connected plane domain avoiding zero, but not on the punctured plane. Identify the relevant loop obstruction.
