# Topology

Point-set foundations and algebraic topology, from compactness to homotopy, homology and duality.

## Scope

Selected advanced-undergraduate and beginning-graduate core with advanced gateways. Entries give precise scoped statements and proof ideas, not full proofs or exhaustive courses.

## Assumed background

Sets, maps, equivalence relations, basic real analysis and groups for algebraic topology.

## Learning outcomes

- Keep compactness, separation and countability assumptions distinct.
- Compute homotopy and homology invariants by decomposition.
- Recognize the coefficient and orientation requirements in duality.

## Problems for active study

1. Compute the fundamental group and integral homology of the torus using a CW structure. Explain what information the cup product adds.
2. Show that a continuous bijection from a compact space to a Hausdorff space is a homeomorphism. Identify where each hypothesis enters.
