# Probability theory

Measure-theoretic probability, limit laws, conditional expectation, martingales and stochastic processes.

## Scope

Selected advanced-undergraduate and beginning-graduate core with advanced gateways. Entries give precise scoped statements and proof ideas, not full proofs or exhaustive courses.

## Assumed background

Measure and integration, Lp spaces, elementary real analysis and linear algebra.

## Learning outcomes

- Identify the convergence mode and moment assumptions in each limit law.
- Formulate conditioning as an almost-everywhere integral identity.
- Check filtration, stopping-time and integrability conditions before stochastic calculations.

## Problems for active study

1. Using two independent fair bits and their sum modulo two, prove pairwise independence and exhibit a failure of mutual independence.
2. Verify that B_t^2-t is a martingale, then use bounded stopping times to derive its optional-stopping identity. Explain the extra work needed for an unbounded stopping time.
