# Algebraic geometry

Commutative algebra, varieties and schemes, followed by local properties, divisors and cohomology of curves.

## Scope

Selected advanced-undergraduate and beginning-graduate core with advanced gateways. Entries give precise scoped statements and proof ideas, not full proofs or exhaustive courses.

## Assumed background

Commutative algebra, modules and tensor products, elementary topology and field extensions.

## Learning outcomes

- Translate geometry into coordinate rings and local rings.
- Retain generic points and nilpotents when passing from varieties to schemes.
- Use divisors, line bundles and cohomology with smoothness and properness hypotheses.

## Problems for active study

1. Compare Spec(k) and Spec(k[epsilon]/(epsilon^2)): their points, local rings and tangent spaces.
2. Compute h0 and h1 of O(d) on the projective line for every integer d using Riemann–Roch and duality.
