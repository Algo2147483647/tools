# Number theory

Arithmetic from divisibility to number fields, local fields, prime distribution and rational points.

## Scope

Selected advanced-undergraduate and beginning-graduate core with advanced gateways. Entries give precise scoped statements and proof ideas, not full proofs or exhaustive courses.

## Assumed background

Abstract algebra including rings and fields, elementary group theory, complex analysis for analytic topics.

## Learning outcomes

- Distinguish element factorization, prime-ideal factorization and class groups.
- Connect global arithmetic with completions and ramification.
- Separate proven distribution theorems from stronger error estimates and conjectures.

## Problems for active study

1. Find the full ring of integers and field discriminant of Q(sqrt(5)); compare with the order Z[sqrt(5)].
2. Lift the root 3 of x^2-2 modulo 7 to a root modulo 49 and then modulo 343 using Hensel's lemma.

- **FT** — J. S. Milne. [Fields and Galois Theory](https://www.jmilne.org/math/CourseNotes/FT.pdf). Version 5.10, 2022.
- **EC** — J. S. Milne. [Elliptic Curves](https://www.jmilne.org/math/Books/EC2.pdf). Second edition, World Scientific, 2020; author-hosted text.
