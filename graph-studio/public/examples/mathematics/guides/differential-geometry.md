# Differential geometry

Smooth manifolds, differential forms, Riemannian geometry, curvature and global geometric theorems.

## Scope

Selected advanced-undergraduate and beginning-graduate core with advanced gateways. Entries give precise scoped statements and proof ideas, not full proofs or exhaustive courses.

## Assumed background

Multivariable calculus, linear algebra, elementary ODEs and manifold topology.

## Learning outcomes

- Express differentiation and integration independently of a coordinate chart.
- Distinguish metric, connection and curvature data.
- Connect local differential geometry to global topological restrictions.

## Problems for active study

1. Compute the Gaussian curvature and total curvature of the round sphere of radius R, and check Gauss–Bonnet.
2. Show that the angular one-form on the punctured plane is closed but not exact by integrating around the unit circle.
